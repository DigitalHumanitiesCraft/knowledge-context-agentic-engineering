Promptotyping und Generative AI

KUG Summer School (M3GIM), Kunstuniversität Graz

Date 16 and 17 September 2026 Event KUG Summer School (M3GIM), Kunstuniversität Graz Audience participants without LLM or programming background Teaching language German Status planned

Specialisation

Two days at introductory level in German. The instance drops the deep knowledge-modelling material of the Full Slide Deck for this audience, meaning the knowledge transformations, the four-axis model, the bootstrap sequence and the layer model, and keeps the slides that ground the checking principle and the idea of a knowledge base. Day one carries the fundamentals and the first two hands-on units, day two carries Promptotyping as the method block together with the remaining exercises and closes on a short governance outlook.

The case material is the estate of the mezzo-soprano Ira Malaniuk in the archive of the Kunstuniversität Graz. Four exercises build on that one holding, each stage changing the technique while the domain stays constant, ordered so that table and text work precede image and map. The exercise excerpts are drawn verbatim from six index tables of the estate, including their real defects, among them competing life-date formats, a duplicate that only context knowledge resolves, and a fabricated authority identifier that serves as the bait for the checking rule the prompt carries.

Participants stay in the browser for both days. The text tasks run through a hosted chat interface and the transcription task through a separate vision environment, so nothing has to be installed and no agent harness is operated. Whether the vision task really needs its own environment is an open preparation item rather than a settled decision.

Preparation is further advanced than the thin profile in the repository suggests. A complete slide-text derivation exists under the delta model, meaning every slide is either a named reference to a slide of the Full Slide Deck with the cut stated or fully written text with speaker notes and sources, and the four hands-on units carry their prompts verbatim. Both await acceptance before a deck is produced. Two of the three digitisates for the transcription exercise are still requested from the project team, so that ladder currently holds its first step. The audience line above comes from the register; the underlying documents record audience, participant count and prior knowledge as open, so read the line as provisional.

Hands-on chain

  1. Cleaned person index from a prepared spreadsheet with seven defect classes, worked against an explicit checking rule.
  2. Controlled tuple schema of type, name and role over the verbatim text of a 1942 concert poster, including the line that carries historical weight and produces no tuple.
  3. Recognition ladder over three sources of rising difficulty, with the poster text from the previous step as the reference transcription.
  4. Places and dates normalised, an authority identifier proposed under the same checking rule and looked up by hand, then a minimal static map from the resulting table.
