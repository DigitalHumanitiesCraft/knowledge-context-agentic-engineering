Abstract

Three connected units introduce agentic knowledge work beyond academia. Participants configure an AI harness, prepare files and project knowledge, define control points and develop a small application from explicit requirements. A synthetic institutional scenario provides common material while allowing participants to transfer the workflow to their own professional context.

Learning objectives

  • Configure an AI harness and complete a bounded first task.
  • Prepare files and maintained knowledge for extended agent work.
  • Define human control points and verify results against existing holdings.
  • Develop and assess a small application from explicit requirements.

Outline

  1. Unit oneSetup, terminal and agentic foundations
  2. Unit twoFiles, knowledge base and controlled workflows
  3. Unit threeLight coding and a small application
