Knowledge, Context und Agentic Engineering für die Forschung

A source-to-edition workflow with medieval charters

Event Student Council of Medieval Studies, Heidelberg University Audience Students and early-career researchers in medieval studies Material German Spoken language German Licence Page text and original course material · CC BY 4.0

Abstract

The workshop follows a small group of medieval charters through handwriting recognition, TEI encoding, entity indexing and publication as a static edition. Participants work inside an AI harness, build structured project knowledge and compare generated transcriptions with facsimiles and prepared TEI data. The exercise makes verification and source relations part of the implementation workflow.

Outline

  1. FoundationsLarge language models, AI agents and the working environment
  2. Project knowledgeInstruction layer and knowledge documents
  3. SourcesHandwriting recognition checked against facsimiles
  4. DataTEI encoding and indexes of persons and organisations
  5. ArtefactA small static scholarly edition

Learning objectives

  • Build a small repository with explicit project knowledge.
  • Assess model-generated handwriting recognition against source evidence.
  • Represent checked text and entities in TEI XML.
  • Publish the resulting data through a small static edition.
