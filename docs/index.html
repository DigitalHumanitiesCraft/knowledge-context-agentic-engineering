Knowledge, Context and Agentic Engineering for Knowledge Work

Christopher Pollin·Digital Humanities Craft

A teaching line on working with frontier language models inside AI harnesses, for everyone doing computer-based, data-driven knowledge work. The corpus is maintained once in five modules, and every workshop below is a documented specialisation of it.

Full Slide Deck and Full Lecture Notes

The corpus exists once, as the Full Slide Deck and the Full Lecture Notes, cut into five modules and maintained in one place. A workshop is a recipe over that corpus, meaning a documented selection of modules with its own depth, case material, teaching language and hands-on work. What a given instance selects is recorded in its profile and shown on its card as a module badge row, which the key in the workshop section reads out.

  • Full Slide Deck Google Slides, all five modules, the source every workshop deck is cut from
  • Full Lecture Notes in preparation Two language versions held in the repository, German and English, with the English text in progress
  • Unit-level structure beneath the five modules in preparation The five modules are the maintained cut; the finer cut into units is a draft

Workshops

Seven instances along the timeline, grouped by month, each with its own module selection. The key states which colour belongs to which module and how a badge reads.

  • 1Understanding Large Language Models

    Capability landscape, jagged capability, latent program space, the assistant character

  • 2Prompt Engineering

    The four-part prompt structure, the prompt as a bounded specification, practical principles

  • 3Knowledge and Context Engineering

    Knowledge documents, the working context, research data and provenance

  • 4Agentic Engineering

    Harness and agentic loop, Promptotyping, verification, hands-on case studies

  • 5Critical Perspectives and Governance

    Governance and decision rights, Research Mission Control, openness, societal implications
