01 “Understanding” Large Language Models

Assistant Character Assistentenfigur
The conversational persona a provider shapes and stabilises through training, runtime instructions, policy layers and product design, separate from the underlying model.
Asymmetric Amplification Asymmetrische Amplifikation
The thesis that frontier LLMs strengthen human capability unevenly, chiefly where knowledge is externalised, objects digitally represented, actions run through software and feedback is meaningful.
Capability Frontier Leistungsgrenze aktueller Modelle
The current outer edge of measurable model capability across reasoning, coding, agentic tasks, speed and cost, located through continuously updated comparisons.
Confabulation Konfabulation
The production of plausible but unsupported claims, the term the material uses for this behaviour in place of hallucination.
Constitution, Character Training and System Prompt Constitution, Character Training und Systeminstruktionen
Three separate layers, a training artefact such as a Constitution, character training that stabilises dispositions, and a system prompt operating at runtime within one deployment.
Contextually Plausible Reading kontextuell plausible Lesung
A transcription failure of multimodal LLMs in which the reading fits the linguistic context while the visual evidence of the source supports a different one.
Embedding and Contextual Representation Embedding und kontextuelle Repräsentation
The mapping of tokens into a high-dimensional vector space and the repeated transformation of these vectors into representations dependent on surrounding tokens and task.
Emergent Capability Emergenz
A capability appearing without separate training, an open question for zero-shot handwriting recognition since proprietary systems do not disclose scale and training composition.
Frontier Model Frontier-LLM
A currently leading LLM whose combined multimodality, reasoning, coding, tool use and agentic capability matter for computational research, though not necessarily appropriate for every task.
In-Context Learning
The ability of an LLM to adapt its behaviour to instructions, examples and information in the current context without any change to its parameters.
Jagged Capability (Jagged Alien Intelligence) Jagged Alien Intelligence
A capability profile in which an LLM solves extremely difficult tasks while failing on neighbouring tasks that look simpler to a human observer.
Large Language Model
A neural network trained on large tokenised collections to estimate the next-token probability distribution given its context, with probabilistic output across repeated runs.
Latent Program Space
The space of learned transformations an LLM can activate depending on input and context, read as a conceptual model of learned processing routines.
Mechanistic Interpretability Mechanistische Interpretierbarkeit
Research into the internal computations through which an LLM produces behaviour, invoked only for the claim that model computation is structured and input-dependent.
Midtraining (Model Spec Midtraining) Midtraining
A proposed stage between pretraining and alignment training that trains a model on synthetic documents about a Model Spec or Constitution, shaping how alignment generalises.
Next Token Prediction
The training objective of autoregressive language modelling, which asks which continuation is probable and is kept separate from the capabilities acquired while optimising it.
Parametric Knowledge parametrisches Wissen
Knowledge an LLM can produce because its parameters encode statistical structure from training material, with no addressable copy of training documents and no direct provenance.
Posttraining Post-Training
The training stages that shape how the learned repertoire is expressed in an assistant, through instruction tuning, preference learning, reinforcement learning and related methods.
Pretraining Pre-Training
The training stage on large heterogeneous corpora that builds broad linguistic, conceptual and procedural representations, in which knowledge and capability remain entangled.
Program Query Programmabfrage
The prompt as an address into the learned repertoire, where one part steers towards a region of it and another supplies the material to process.
Reasoning and Test-Time Compute Reasoning
The allocation of additional computation during inference before a final answer, shifting part of the search burden from external prompt variation into one model invocation.
Sycophancy
The tendency of an LLM to align an answer with a belief the user has expressed, even where this reduces factual accuracy.
Task-Completion Time Horizon Aufgabenhorizont
The duration of software tasks an agent can complete at a specified success probability, one signal among several and no general measure of research capability.
Token and Tokenization Token und Tokenisierung
The discrete units into which a tokenizer converts character sequences, so that context limits, input cost and output length are measured in tokens.
Transformer Architecture and Self-Attention Transformer-Modell
The layered architecture in which attention lets positions of a sequence influence one another, giving each token a context-dependent representation.
Vector Program Vektorprogramm
A learned, distributed transformation across high-dimensional representations that realises one capability or behaviour, distinct from a discrete symbolic program stored inside the model.
Vision-Language Model Vision-Language-Modell
A model processing visual and linguistic information within one task, so it can transcribe a facsimile without being a dedicated OCR or HTR system.

02 Prompt Engineering

Bounded Specification Begrenzte Spezifikation
A prompt specifying a current task inside an existing knowledge and working context, pointing at persistent project rules so that it stays compact and precise.
Clarifying Questions Rückfragen
A prompting strategy in which the model first asks a few questions about goal and context before it starts working.
Milestone Meilenstein
A checkpoint at which an AI agent stops until a person has checked and released the intermediate result.
Persona Prompting (Persona Engineering) Persona Prompting
A worked-out perspective with background, experience, goals, constraints and usage situation, used to review an artefact and yielding hypotheses about users without empirical user data.
Prompt
The input sequence supplied for one model call, which may contain task, source material, context, requirements, constraints, examples, procedural instructions and output specifications.
Prompt Engineering
The iterative development of individual prompts by changing their content, structure or techniques, addressing one input sequence and therefore narrower than context engineering.
Prompt Sensitivity Promptwirkungen
The finding that LLM output can shift with linguistic details of little task relevance, so prompting effects are local and treated as experimental interventions.
Role Prompting
A brief functional assignment of a role to an LLM, which can influence terminology, style, perspective and detail while adding no factual knowledge.
Self-Revision
A staged interaction in which an LLM drafts, checks the draft against explicit criteria and revises only confirmed findings, surfacing errors without constituting independent verification.
Structured Output Strukturierte Ausgaben
Output constrained to a defined format such as JSON or XML, enabling deterministic checks of syntax and structure that prove nothing about scholarly adequacy.
Task, Context, Rules, Output
A prompt structure separating task, needed source or project information, governing constraints and output form, so that each part can be revised independently.

03 Knowledge and Context Engineering

Agent Memory (Auto Memory) Memory
Notes an AI agent writes itself from its work and reloads in later sessions, serving as a recall aid without binding force.
Agent Skill and Progressive Disclosure Agent Skill
A reusable folder with SKILL.md and resources for one recurring task class, loaded in stages so only name and description occupy context until relevant.
Context Compression
Reduction of the information entering an active context through selecting relevant passages, summarising, removing repetition, aggregating data, keeping representative examples or compacting working history.
Context Engineering
The systematic selection, organisation, maintenance and provision of the task-specific information state for a model call or an agentic run.
Context Rot
Observed degradation in an LLM's ability to retrieve and apply relevant information as the active context grows longer, denser or more distracting.
Context Window
The finite token-based processing space of one model call, whose nominal size is a technical limit that sets no target for the amount of input.
Dense and Sufficient Context Dichter und hinreichender Kontext
The target for a working context, as bounded as possible while carrying every distinction the task requires, including provenance, conditions and uncertainty.
Distillation
Transforming an understanding into a selective, structured and inspectable representation preserving what a given purpose requires, operating on documents, unlike knowledge distillation in machine learning.
Docling
An open-source tool that converts PDFs and other documents locally into structured Markdown or JSON for knowledge work with agents.
Frontmatter
A YAML block of key-value pairs at the top of a Markdown file that records metadata such as type, date, status and tags.
Instruction File Instruktionsdatei
A persistent Markdown file such as AGENTS.md or CLAUDE.md that sets recurring rules for agentic work in a project and loads at session start.
Knowledge Acquisition
The elicitation and explicitation of knowledge from documents and data and from the implicit knowledge of people and organisations, through analysis, interviews, workshops and observation.
Knowledge Document Wissensdokument
A bounded, structured and revisable representation of relevant knowledge, distilled from a larger body, checked by people and usable as context by LLM-based systems.
Knowledge Engineering
The purpose-bound construction and maintenance of explicit, inspectable, revisable project knowledge, making concepts, rules, decisions, constraints and uncertainties visible enough for critique and continuation.
Knowledge Modelling Wissensmodellierung
The classical construction of a knowledge base by identifying, formalising and querying domain concepts, shifted in the material towards structured natural language with light metadata.
Knowledge-Base Governance and Curation Governance und Kuration
Governance sets the rules for building, changing and using a knowledge base, and curation applies them to its holdings, with substantive changes under human responsibility.
Map of Content (MOC)
A curated entry note that gathers annotated links to related documents in a subject area and serves agents as navigation.
Markdown as Serialization Markdown als technische Repräsentation
The knowledge document as conceptual artefact and Markdown as one possible representation, chosen for openness, versionability, linkability and readability by people and LLMs.
Over-Distillation Überdestillation
The counter-risk of distillation, in which provenance, uncertainty or actionable detail is lost and several alternative statements collapse into one apparently unambiguous rule.
Personal Information Management
The study of how people acquire, organise, maintain, retrieve, use and share information across formats and locations, with fragmentation as its core problem.
Project Knowledge Base
The persistent, inspectable and revisable body of documented project knowledge on data, purpose, requirements, decisions and uncertainties, describing sources and research data without replacing them.
Promptotyping Documents (Declarative, Process and Action Documents) Promptotyping Documents
Knowledge documents classified by the knowledge they carry, about the subject (declarative), the course of work (process) or acting (action).
Tacit Knowledge and Externalisation Implizites Wissen
Knowledge held by people, teams or institutions without inspectable documentation, whose gap to operationally available knowledge is where knowledge engineering starts.
Tag and Tag Taxonomy Tag und Tag-Taxonomie
A controlled vocabulary of non-overlapping tag categories, within which new tags may arise, keeping tagging consistent across a vault.
Vault (Obsidian Vault) Vault
A local folder of Markdown files that Obsidian opens as a linked knowledge collection, readable by people and AI agents alike.
Wikilink
An internal link written as a note name in double square brackets, connecting documents in a vault.
Working Context
The task-specific configuration of information, documents, data access, instructions, tool descriptions, permissions, state and feedback assembled for one assignment, not all of which becomes tokens.

04 Agentic Engineering

Agent2Agent Protocol (A2A) Agent2Agent (A2A)
An open protocol for discovery, task management and result exchange among independent agents without exposing their internal memory, tools or logic, complementing MCP's agent-to-tool standard.
Agentic Engineering
The systematic organisation and control of multi-step agentic work, from task delimitation and tool use through intermediate states and handovers to stopping, escalation and checking.
Agentic Execution Loop Agentische Ausführungsschleife
The repeated cycle of registering state, planning a bounded step, acting, observing and updating the approach, bound to requirements, permissions, stopping conditions and intervention points.
Agents Rule of Two Rule of Two
A security rule allowing an AI agent at most two of untrusted input, private data access and external action per session.
AI Agent and Agentic AI AI Agent und Agentic AI
An agent pursues a goal across several model and tool calls, observing results and updating its approach, while agentic AI names the wider multi-agent paradigm.
AI Harness
The software layer through which an LLM-based agent receives context, calls tools, accesses files and processes feedback, providing technical control without deciding scholarly adequacy.
Autonomy Autonomie
The extent of work performed between two human interventions, a reading compatible with full human control and narrower than the classical agent-systems property.
Compound System (Model, Harness and Environment) Verbundsystem
Agentic capability as a joint property of model, harness and environment, making the whole system of context, tools, state, permissions and feedback the analytical unit.
Deterministic and Probabilistic Tasks (Script versus LLM Division) Deterministische und probabilistische Aufgaben
The division of tasks into those a script solves with one correct answer and those requiring the judgment of an LLM or person.
Epistemic Infrastructure Epistemische Infrastruktur
Knowledge documents, data, schemas, tests, tools, agents and human judgment arranged so that a generated representation becomes inspectable, validatable and acceptable for a stated purpose.
Fancy Research Tools Fancy (Research) Tools
Small browser-based research tools that coding agents build from a maintained knowledge base, checked and released by people.
Harness Engineering
The design and checking of the environment in which an AI agent works, including its tools, knowledge, permissions and controls.
Hook
A script the AI harness runs automatically at a defined event, which can block an agent's action independently of the model.
Intervention Point and Escalation Condition Menschliche Interventionspunkte und Eskalationsbedingungen
The defined places where an agentic run hands back to a person, such as contradictory requirements, hard-to-reverse changes, sensitive resources, disciplinary modelling decisions and acceptance.
Least-Privilege Tool Access Prinzip der geringsten erforderlichen Berechtigung
The rule that tool and file access is granted at the minimum required scope, with sources read-only and publication or deployment requiring explicit release.
Loop Engineering
The design of the system that triggers an AI agent and checks its results with fixed tests before a person releases them.
Model Context Protocol (MCP)
An open standard connecting AI applications with external tools, data sources and workflows, which addresses integration and decides nothing about a resource's relevance or reliability.
Model Routing
The assignment of different phases of an agentic workflow to different models and inference budgets, an engineering decision that changes as models change.
Permission Mode
A harness setting that determines which actions an AI agent may take without asking, and thus how much autonomy it has.
Promptotype
The documented, referenceable state of one iteration, comprising knowledge base, artefact, data state, checks, open questions and bounded grounds of acceptance, to which acceptance attaches.
Promptotyping
An iterative, document-based method for developing project-specific digital research artefacts with LLM-based agents, in which project understanding, artefact, checks and grounds of acceptance develop together.
Promptotyping Phase Model Phasenmodell des Promptotyping
The method's recurring work forms, preparing sources and workspace, exploring what data permit, distilling understanding into documents and implementing the artefact to expose knowledge gaps.
Scholar-Centred Design
The orientation of development towards the research practices, interpretative tasks and responsibilities of scholars, asking which scholarly distinctions an interface keeps visible.
Spec-Driven Development spezifikationsgetriebene Entwicklung
The placement of an explicit specification, contextualised by the maintained knowledge base, between exploratory discussion and implementation, so that a reviewable object precedes code.
Subagent
A delegated agent instance with a bounded partial task, fresh context and defined return format, keeping intermediate information out of the parent context.
Tool Use Werkzeugnutzung
The connection of an LLM to tools and resources such as files, terminal, code execution and validators, most valuable where tools report consequences of actions.
Trajectory Arbeitstrajektorie
The complete sequence of observations, decisions, tool calls, file modifications, execution results and follow-up actions of an agentic run, which becomes the object of engineering.
User Story
A requirements format stating who acts, which capability is required and why it matters, bridging structured research data and an implementable artefact.
Write-back
The defined operation of returning examined findings into the responsible documents, so new knowledge stays out of chat, code and individual memory.

05 Critical Perspectives and Governance

Acceptance
The responsible decision to use an identified state for a named purpose, separate from technical verification and from publication.
AI Literacy Obligation (Art. 4) KI-Kompetenz
The duty to ensure the skills, knowledge and understanding for informed decisions about AI systems, one of two obligations binding a research institution using AI.
AI Slop and De-Slopification AI Slop und De-Slopification
Generic elements LLMs add unasked, such as decorative figures or promotional wording, and their deliberate removal in each iteration.
AI Watermarking Wasserzeichen
A hidden statistical or pixel-level mark indicating AI-generated text or images, detectable with a key and indicative of origin only.
Candidate Representation Kandidatenrepräsentation
The status of an LLM output before assessment, valuable as a plausible structured proposal while carrying no evidence of source fidelity.
Critical Cyber Capability kritische Cyberfähigkeiten
The capability threshold at which a provider cannot exclude a model developing zero-day exploits or running novel end-to-end attacks with minimal human direction.
Critical Expert
The responsible disciplinary authority in a project, defined by function, which decides which reading is defensible and whether a state is accepted for a purpose.
Deployer (Art. 3) Betreiber
A natural or legal person using an AI system under their own authority in a professional capacity, excluding purely private non-professional use.
Deterministic Check Deterministische Prüfung
A reproducible test over an explicit representation, such as recomputing table sums or schema validation, revealing internal inconsistency without establishing correct interpretation of the source.
Evaluation
Measurement or assessment of outputs, models or workflows against explicit criteria, quantitative or qualitative, including benchmark scores, error rates and structured expert review.
General-Purpose AI Model (GPAI) GPAI-Modell
The separate Chapter V category, whose providers owe documentation, downstream information, a copyright policy and a training-data summary, with further obligations under presumed systemic risk.
Governance Dilemma of Diffusion and Concentration Governance-Dilemma zwischen Diffusion und Konzentration
The tension, stated as the author's assessment, that open-weight systems can spread offensive capability while proprietary frontier systems concentrate capability within few companies and states.
High-Risk System Hochrisiko-System
The classification with the most demanding compliance regime, reached as a safety component under Annex I or a standalone system in an Annex III domain.
Local Deployment lokaler Betrieb
Running model inference on hardware the researcher or institution controls, which can increase control over data and configuration while shifting operational responsibility to the institution.
Model-Assisted Review Agentisches Review
The inspection of an LLM's output by a second LLM with source and tool access, which directs attention, though its agreement is no scholarly validation.
Open Source AI Definition Open-Source-KI-Definition
The definition requiring freedoms to use, study, modify and share an AI system plus access to training-data information, source code and parameters.
Open-Washing
The presentation of an AI system as open while significant restrictions on accessibility or reproducibility remain.
Open-Weight Model Open-Weight-Modell
A model whose trained parameters are available under a specified licence for download, adaptation and independent execution, kept strictly separate from open source.
Openness as a Multidimensional Property mehrdimensionale Offenheit
Openness read as separately gradable dimensions covering training data, documentation, training code, architecture, parameters, licences and access conditions, replacing a binary open or closed classification.
Prohibited Practices (Art. 5) verbotene Praktiken
The eight absolutely prohibited categories of AI practice, such as manipulation, social scoring and workplace emotion recognition, the second obligation relevant to a using institution.
Provider (Art. 3(3)) Anbieter
A natural or legal person developing an AI system or GPAI model and placing it on the market or into service under their own name.
Research Exemption (Art. 2(6)) Forschungsausnahme
The exemption for AI systems developed and used solely for scientific research before market placement, ending with the transition to deployment whatever the research subject.
Risk-Based Approach risikobasierter Ansatz
The organising principle of the AI Act, a four-tier classification into unacceptable, high, limited and minimal risk, where the tier determines the intensity of obligations.
Technical Verification Technische Verifikation
The check whether an artefact conforms to specified or formalised requirements, through schemas, tests and executable criteria, and therefore largely automatable.
Validation (Scholarly or Domain Validation) Fachliche und wissenschaftliche Validierung
The check whether a representation, interpretation or artefact is adequate to source, purpose and disciplinary context, which requires domain expertise and resists deterministic testing.
Verification Levels Prüfstufen
The earlier umbrella name for the ordered checks on an artefact, kept as pointer to separate entries for procedures, the Critical Expert role and acceptance.

General and cross-cutting

AI Readiness KI-Bereitschaft von Forschungsdaten
Whether research data are sufficiently structured, documented and governed for AI-supported workflows, including machine-readable representations, clear provenance and information for assessing quality and bias.
Applied Generative AI Angewandte Generative KI
The application and adaptation of generative AI methods to problems of a specific research domain, integrating generative models into existing research practice.
Capta
The argument that humanities data are constructed through situated acts of observation and representation, so a transcription already carries decisions about legibility and textual structure.
Knowledge Work Wissensarbeit
Work paid for applying theoretical knowledge and exercising judgment, determined by the worker's own definition of the task and by continuous learning.
Provenance Provenienz
The documented relation between a source, the operations applied to it and the resulting representation, kept separate from the question of validity.
Research Data Forschungsdaten
Representations of research objects, sources, observations or processes produced through scholarly work, shaped by research decisions and dependent on documentation and context.
Research Data Workflow Forschungsdaten-Workflow
The organised process creating, transforming, enriching, assessing and preparing research data for scholarly use, within which automated pipelines perform individual transformations.
