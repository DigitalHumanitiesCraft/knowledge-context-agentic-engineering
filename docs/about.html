About & Imprint

Knowledge, Context and Agentic Engineering for Knowledge Work is a teaching line developed and maintained by Digital Humanities Craft. This site provides access to the shared material, documented workshop specialisations and preparation resources. Their versioned sources are available on GitHub.

Why the material is public

Knowledge about AI-supported knowledge work is easier to assess when its sources, terms and teaching materials are visible. The platform therefore publishes the shared material and documents which modules individual workshops select and how they adapt them. Participants can return to the resources after an event. Other readers can inspect the basis of the teaching.

How the work is funded

DHCraft develops this teaching line as part of its commercial work. Commissioned workshops finance the preparation of new examples, editorial revision, technical implementation and ongoing maintenance of the publicly accessible material. Each organisation receives a version adapted to its field, tasks and participants. Generally applicable results are incorporated into the shared material.

Why the material continues to change

Large Language Models, AI harnesses and the practices around them develop quickly. The material is revised when product behaviour, technical capabilities, terminology or the evidence base changes. Versioned source files and explicit preparation states show which resources are available and which remain under development.

How workshops relate to the shared material

The Full Slide Deck provides the reference structure and the source from which workshop-specific decks are assembled. The Full Lecture Notes are its written-out counterpart, with the explanations and context needed for reading and later reference. The Video Playlist provides a curated audiovisual route from foundations to more complex topics. Each workshop selects and rearranges modules from the slide deck for a specific domain, then adds relevant examples and exercises. Shared conceptual changes are reconciled across the slide deck and lecture notes before they enter later workshop versions.
