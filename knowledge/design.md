# Platform Design

Design stance and system values for `docs/`. The platform addresses a professional, international audience from research and industry. The maintained Slide Deck, Lecture Notes and playlist are the main resources; Preparation uses its existing Google Slides deck.

## Landing-page hierarchy

The landing page begins with a compact two-column hero. Title and a substantive abstract occupy the main reading column. A second, shorter paragraph states why Preparation exists and links directly to the language-matched page. The five module titles form a compact, non-interactive Full Slide Deck index in the right column. Each module carries one portable line icon beside its number: a jagged capability boundary inside a black box, a selected coordinate in latent space, maintained documents with a context aperture, coordinated agents inside a tool-bearing harness, and a claim inspected before an authorisation gate. Text remains the semantic and accessible source, while colour and icon add independent visual distinctions. Every row uses one fixed icon-and-number column and one flexible title column; the title aligns to the first line of the number, wraps with a line height of approximately 1.2 and retains regular separation from the next row. The index is vertically centred against the complete hero copy and offset slightly toward the right edge. Narrow viewports place it below the abstract and reset the offset. The abstract identifies the subject, the five concept families, the shared teaching material and the derivation of workshops as documented specialisations. Its key terms carry concise language-matched definitions. A term remains in the text colour and normal weight, while a fine underline in its module colour identifies its conceptual location and distinguishes it from an ordinary hyperlink. Its definition appears beside the term on pointer hover, keyboard focus or touch. The right column remains dedicated to the Full Slide Deck index.

The landing fragments `module-01` through `module-05` remain compatibility targets for workshop markers and external links. Numbered modules describe the Full Slide Deck structure and do not create a second public page hierarchy.

Teaching Materials follows the hero without an intervening overview block or large section gap. The Full Slide Deck, the English and German Full Lecture Notes and the Video Playlist occupy three equal resource cards. Each card states the function of its holding rather than a count of modules. The Full Slide Deck card names the complete overview in slide form that serves as reference work and as the source for workshop-specific decks. The Full Lecture Notes card names the written-out counterpart of the slides with its explanations and context for reading and reference. The compact Video Playlist description is `An ordered video sequence from foundational to advanced topics, maintained and expanded over time.` in English and `Eine geordnete Videoreihe von den Grundlagen zu vertiefenden Themen, die laufend gepflegt und erweitert wird.` in German. Authorship and longer editorial provenance remain in the About pages and knowledge records. Each card begins with a compact horizontal summary that places a small purpose-specific line mark beside its title and description. Format actions span the card below this summary. The Full Slide Deck and each Full Lecture Notes language row reserve one action place for Google, Markdown and PDF. Repeated action labels stay short enough to remain on one line, while their accessible names identify the exact Google destination. Available formats are links, while `null` formats appear as quiet preparation states. The lecture-notes mark carries one compact segmented language capsule with neutral text and a small palette dot for each maintained version. The video mark connects one player surface with the five module colours.

Preparation follows Teaching Materials as a single reading column with a short description and a link to the language-matched page. The Video Library follows directly, retaining native disclosure groups, the registered order and external-thumbnail disclosure. Workshop timelines, event cards and Learning Routes are removed. Stable module anchors and the material, preparation and video anchors remain; removed route anchors have no internal callers.

## System values

- **Ground**: paper white as one consistent page ground, near-black typography, generous whitespace; light theme only (operator decision 2026-08-20).
- **Accent**: turquoise `#0A7E7C` as the primary accent for links, active states and motif highlights; the motif line art stays turquoise-only.
- **Identity palette**: four accents derived from the watercolor logo, orange `#C87000`, blue `#5B8FC4`, green `#7C9200`, violet `#8140B8`. The interface applies them to the five module marks (turquoise 01, orange 02, blue 03, violet 04, green 05, as implemented in `docs/assets/site.css`). Cover illustrations may use the same colors semantically inside their depicted objects. Text-carrying colors must pass WCAG AA, pure marks at least 3:1.
- **Brand**: DHCraft watercolor logo (standard variant from `DigitalHumanitiesCraft/brand-assets`), set sparingly.
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk throughout with system fallback (operator decision 2026-08-20); IBM Plex Mono for prompts and code.
- **Hierarchy**: at most two text levels per section, one heading and body. Preparation places its introduction and direct link before the viewer.
- **Imagery**: the shared resources and live Preparation deck provide the current visual surfaces. Historical workshop covers remain unused assets.
- **Cards**: resource cards present the maintained holdings and their available formats. Video cards remain inside native module disclosure groups.
- **Chrome**: header site identity, top navigation, EN/DE control and footer are one binding public-page template; the favicon set in `docs/assets/favicon/` is committed and every page links it. The top navigation carries three links in the fixed order `Preparation / Vorbereitung`, `About & Imprint / Über & Impressum` and `GitHub`. Only the Preparation pages mark the Preparation link `aria-current="page"` and only the About pages mark the combined entry; landing pages mark none. Local targets resolve at the depth of their directory. Local detail pages retain language-matched backlinks into the teaching platform. Every page begins with a keyboard-reachable skip link whose target is the main content. The header logo link carries its own accessible publisher name; the footer logo stays decorative beside the visible publisher block. Every public page carries stylesheet version `20260826-2`, so newly installed markup cannot render against an older cached design system. Page chrome is not re-invented per page.
- **Current topology**: the public tree contains reciprocal English and German landing, Preparation and About pages. Event pages and Learning Routes are retired. Formal validation and observed browser behaviour are recorded in the journal; author acceptance and release remain separate.
- **Site identity**: the header carries one `site-identity` group with two adjacent links that have distinct destinations and distinct accessible names. The compact DHCraft logo is the only header element leading to `https://dhcraft.org`, and its accessible name names the publisher website in the page language. Beside it the complete teaching-line title is the visible site name in both interface languages and links to the language-matched landing page, resolved at the depth of its own directory, using `index.html` or `index.de.html`. The visible wordmark `Digital Humanities Craft` leaves the header, because publisher identity stays legible through the accessible name of the logo and the publisher block of the footer. The logo stays visually subordinate to the title. Both links are separate keyboard stops with their own visible focus indicator and keep at least 44 pixels on coarse pointers. The title wraps to a second line where the width requires it and is never truncated into an unexplained acronym; at narrow widths the reading order stays logo before title and the navigation may occupy its own row. The landing-page title may link to the current page and carries no `aria-current`, because the site navigation already marks the current location where one applies. Substantive Research Director acceptance remains separate from technical verification.

The navigation retains the combined local About and Imprint entry, teaching material, Promptotyping, Research Mission Control or Forschungsleitstelle, `CC BY 4.0` and the canonical legal notice. ## Interface contract

`docs/assets/site.css` contains the executable token values; this document records their roles and the reasons for their use. The external `UI-DESIGN.md` supplied on 2026-08-25 was evaluated as a design-system audit and has been consolidated here where it matches the accepted interface. Its proposals for a separate design document, point-only module marks, a material matrix, card-wide workshop links, fixed 768/1024 breakpoints and a top-aligned hero index are superseded by the implemented decisions above.

The type system has two semantic families. Space Grotesk carries reading text, titles and controls. IBM Plex Mono carries dates, sequence numbers, module numbers, category labels, code and prompt surfaces. The responsive scale is defined by `--text-xs` through `--text-hero`; body copy resolves between 16 and 17 pixels, section titles between 22 and 28 pixels, subpage titles between 28 and 40 pixels and the landing title between 32 and 56 pixels. Prose uses a 60-character measure. Uppercase micro-labels are reserved for metadata and never substitute for headings.

The spatial system uses an eight-pixel base from `--s1` through `--s7`, a maximum page width of 1160 pixels and section gaps that remain larger than the internal gaps of their components. The responsive rules are content-specific instead of being treated as device classes. The principal transitions occur at 1180, 1020, 860, 780 and 640 pixels. The 860-pixel boundary changes the main landing and video grids; the 780-pixel boundary changes tutorial navigation; the 640-pixel boundary produces the single-column video library and the narrowest component layouts. Every grid child can shrink through `min-width: 0`, and no component may create page-level horizontal scrolling.

Interactive elements have three visual roles. Inline links are underlined and identify destinations in prose or local navigation. Bordered actions expose an available external or downloadable resource. Dark filled controls are reserved for the primary action inside a focused tutorial surface. Unavailable formats are labelled states rather than links or button-shaped controls. Focus uses the shared two-pixel turquoise outline with a three-pixel offset; hover never supplies information that keyboard focus cannot reveal.

White bordered resource cards present the maintained holdings and their formats. Video cards are subordinate items inside a module disclosure group. Media surfaces preserve their intrinsic or declared ratio; slide viewers and video previews use 16:9.

Material and Preparation links originate in `docs/data/workshops.json`; its workshop records retain provenance without generating public pages. Playlist state originates in `docs/data/videos.json`. Paired static HTML is updated with these sources in the same maintenance step. Runtime JavaScript does not repair inconsistent content. HTML supplies one `h1`, native disclosure controls and one main-content target per page. Module colour, icon and number reinforce one another; unavailable formats include visible text.
