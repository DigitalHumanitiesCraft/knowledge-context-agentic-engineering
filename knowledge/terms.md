# Terms

The canonical glossary of the teaching line *Knowledge, Context and Agentic Engineering for Knowledge Work*. It fixes the wording of the terms that the material uses as load-bearing concepts, so that lecture notes, slide texts and workshop profiles are written and revised against one vocabulary. Entries are ordered by the five modules of the material, preceded by the terms that hold across all of them. Each entry gives the English canonical term, the German equivalent, a definition grounded in the material rather than in general usage, the place in the material where the term does its work, and a literature anchor where a citable source exists. Its last fields are public, `*Short.*` and `*Kurz.*` with one sentence each in English and German, `*Begriff.*` with the German headword and an optional `*Blog.*` link, and `scripts/build_glossary.py` turns them into the glossary pages of the site, so this file stays the only place where a definition is maintained.

Source labels are abbreviated. CLARIAH means the CLARIAH-AT lecture notes (`workshops/2026-09-25-clariah-at/lecture-notes.md`), UFL the Uni for Life lecture notes (`workshops/2026-11-09-ufl/lecture-notes.md`), Notes EN the English Full Lecture Notes (`script/full-lecture-notes-en.md`), Skriptum DE the German Full Lecture Notes (`script/full-lecture-notes-de.md`), Deck the Full Slide Deck (`slides/full-slide-deck.md`), VetMedAI the workshop script of the delivered Vetmeduni instance of 2026-04-22. That instance is a past one, so its folder no longer lives in the working tree; the file is preserved in git history at `8d50211` as `workshops/2026-04-22-vetmedai-workshop-1/workshop-script.txt`, and the entries grounded in it stay valid. A German rendering counts as attested where a German source carries it and is marked as a proposal where none does. Literature is cited in short form; the full records are listed under References at the end of this document.

Where the material contradicts itself, the glossary states one wording as canonical and records the variant. Six such cuts are marked in place as *Editorial decision 2026-08-20*, and each of them is reversible by the operator.

1. Program Query is the canonical term; Program Key is recorded as a superseded coinage of the earlier matrix.
2. Technical Verification means formal conformity and Validation means domain adequacy; the CLARIAH wording is documented as an instance variant with a mapping note.
3. The knowledge document is defined by the wording of the Full Lecture Notes; the CLARIAH wording is recorded as instance wording, and knowledge document, instruction file and agent skill remain three separate entries.
4. Distillation carries an explicit disambiguation against the established machine-learning sense.
5. Autonomy is defined as the Skriptum defines it, with a note on the classical agent-systems property.
6. The anchor sets gain the AI-harness paper (Zhong and Zhu 2026) and the Promptotyping monograph.

## General and Cross-Cutting

Terms that hold across the five modules. The research-data cluster is placed here provisionally; the CLARIAH profile leaves the module assignment of its opening sections undecided, and the cluster would sit equally well at the head of module 1.

- **Knowledge Work.** German Wissensarbeit (proposal). Work paid for the application of theoretical knowledge and the exercise of judgment rather than for manual execution or supervision, with the worker's own definition of the task and continuous learning as its determining conditions. *Source.* Title of the teaching line and Deck title; the material generalises its audience to computer-based, data-driven knowledge work. *Literature.* P. F. Drucker 1959; P. F. Drucker 1999 (DOI 10.2307/41165987); Davenport 2005. *Short.* Work paid for applying theoretical knowledge and exercising judgment, determined by the worker's own definition of the task and by continuous learning. *Begriff.* Wissensarbeit. *Kurz.* Arbeit, die für die Anwendung theoretischen Wissens und die Ausübung von Urteilskraft bezahlt wird und von der eigenen Definition der Aufgabe und kontinuierlichem Lernen bestimmt ist.
- **Applied Generative AI.** German Angewandte Generative KI (attested through the working-group name in the CLARIAH footnote). The application and adaptation of generative AI methods and systems to problems of a specific research domain, in the tradition of applied computer science, focused on integrating generative models into existing research practice. It names the disciplinary frame inside which the four engineering layers sit. *Source.* CLARIAH, "Applied Generative AI for Research Data Workflows"; Notes EN, same section. *Literature.* No external canonical source; the scripts anchor the term to the author's own framing and to the DHd working group Angewandte Generative KI in den Digitalen Geisteswissenschaften. *Short.* The application and adaptation of generative AI methods to problems of a specific research domain, integrating generative models into existing research practice. *Begriff.* Angewandte Generative KI. *Kurz.* Die Anwendung und Anpassung generativer KI-Methoden auf Probleme einer bestimmten Forschungsdomäne, mit dem Ziel, generative Modelle in bestehende Forschungspraxis zu integrieren.
- **Research Data.** German Forschungsdaten (Skriptum DE §1; Deck). Representations of research objects, sources, observations or processes that are selected, collected, constructed or produced through scholarly work, shaped by research decisions and dependent on documentation and context for critical assessment, reproducibility and reuse. *Source.* CLARIAH, "Humanities Research Data Are Constructed through Scholarly Workflows"; Notes EN, same section. *Literature.* Geiger 2024 (DOI 10.17175/wp_2023_003_v2); Schöch 2013. *Short.* Representations of research objects, sources, observations or processes produced through scholarly work, shaped by research decisions and dependent on documentation and context. *Begriff.* Forschungsdaten. *Kurz.* Repräsentationen von Forschungsgegenständen, Quellen, Beobachtungen oder Prozessen, die durch wissenschaftliche Arbeit entstehen, von Forschungsentscheidungen geprägt sind und von Dokumentation und Kontext abhängen.
- **Capta.** German Capta, untranslated (absent from the German sources). The argument that humanities data are constructed through situated acts of observation and representation rather than given, so that a transcription already carries decisions about legibility and textual structure. *Source.* CLARIAH, "Humanities Research Data Are Constructed through Scholarly Workflows". *Literature.* J. Drucker 2011 (*Digital Humanities Quarterly* 5.1). *Short.* The argument that humanities data are constructed through situated acts of observation and representation, so a transcription already carries decisions about legibility and textual structure. *Begriff.* Capta. *Kurz.* Das Argument, dass geisteswissenschaftliche Daten durch situierte Akte der Beobachtung und Repräsentation konstruiert werden, sodass schon eine Transkription Entscheidungen über Lesbarkeit und Textstruktur enthält.
- **Research Data Workflow.** German Forschungsdaten-Workflow (proposal); the Skriptum describes the matter as durchgehender Arbeitszusammenhang (§1.4). The organised process through which research materials and data are created, transformed, enriched, assessed and prepared for scholarly use, including source context, scholarly rules, provenance and the procedures for assessment and acceptance. Automated pipelines perform individual transformations inside it. *Source.* CLARIAH, "Humanities Research Data Are Constructed through Scholarly Workflows" and "AI-Supported Research Data Workflows & Epistemic Infrastructures". *Literature.* Definition of the teaching line, positioned against a pipeline reading. *Short.* The organised process creating, transforming, enriching, assessing and preparing research data for scholarly use, within which automated pipelines perform individual transformations. *Begriff.* Forschungsdaten-Workflow. *Kurz.* Der organisierte Prozess, in dem Forschungsdaten erzeugt, transformiert, angereichert, bewertet und für die wissenschaftliche Nutzung aufbereitet werden und innerhalb dessen automatisierte Pipelines einzelne Transformationen ausführen.
- **AI Readiness.** German KI-Bereitschaft von Forschungsdaten (proposal). Whether research data are sufficiently structured, documented and governed for effective use in AI-supported workflows, including machine-readable representations, clear provenance and usage conditions, and the information needed to assess quality, uncertainty and potential bias. *Source.* CLARIAH, "Humanities Research Data Are Constructed through Scholarly Workflows"; Notes EN, same section. *Literature.* Majithia et al. 2026 (DOI 10.1002/aaai.70054); Wilkinson et al. 2016 (DOI 10.1038/sdata.2016.18); MLCommons Croissant; GIDA, CARE Principles. *Short.* Whether research data are sufficiently structured, documented and governed for AI-supported workflows, including machine-readable representations, clear provenance and information for assessing quality and bias. *Begriff.* KI-Bereitschaft von Forschungsdaten. *Kurz.* Das Maß, in dem Forschungsdaten für KI-gestützte Workflows hinreichend strukturiert, dokumentiert und geregelt sind, einschließlich maschinenlesbarer Repräsentationen, klarer Provenienz und Angaben zur Bewertung von Qualität und Bias.
- **Provenance.** German Provenienz (Skriptum DE §1.4, §4.4, §5.4, §5.8, §7.2). The documented relation between a source, the operations applied to it and the resulting representation, required wherever generated artefacts are stored separately from unchanged originals. It is separate from the question of validity. *Source.* Skriptum DE §7.2 and §5.4 as a property of a knowledge document; CLARIAH, "Prepare the Working Environment before the First Prompt". *Literature.* No anchor in the mined texts; the module map records PROV-O and the Web Annotation Data Model as the formal layer of the Skriptum's chapter 1. *Short.* The documented relation between a source, the operations applied to it and the resulting representation, kept separate from the question of validity. *Begriff.* Provenienz. *Kurz.* Die dokumentierte Beziehung zwischen einer Quelle, den auf sie angewandten Operationen und der resultierenden Repräsentation, getrennt von der Frage der Validität.

## 1 Understanding Large Language Models

### Capability landscape

- **Frontier Model.** German Frontier-LLM (Deck). A currently leading general-purpose model whose combination of multimodality, reasoning, coding, tool use and agentic capability the scripts treat as qualitatively relevant for computational research, without claiming that it is the appropriate choice for every task. *Source.* CLARIAH, "Applied Generative AI for Research Data Workflows" and "The Current LLM Capability Landscape"; Notes EN, abstract and "Which Frontier Models and AI Technologies Do You Use?". *Literature.* Anderljung et al. 2023 (arXiv:2307.03718), proposed anchor for the received definition; the scripts use the Artificial Analysis leaderboard as the moving reference point. *Short.* A currently leading LLM whose combined multimodality, reasoning, coding, tool use and agentic capability matter for computational research, though not necessarily appropriate for every task. *Begriff.* Frontier-LLM. *Kurz.* Ein aktuell führendes Allzweck-LLM, dessen Verbindung von Multimodalität, Reasoning, Programmierung, Werkzeugnutzung und agentischer Fähigkeit für computergestützte Forschung relevant ist, auch wenn es nicht für jede Aufgabe geeignet sein muss.
- **Capability Frontier.** German Leistungsgrenze aktueller Modelle (proposal). The current outer edge of measurable model capability across reasoning, coding, agentic tasks, speed and cost, located through continuously updated comparisons rather than through a single benchmark score. *Source.* CLARIAH, "The Current LLM Capability Landscape" and "Frontier LLMs Asymmetrically Amplify Computational Research". *Literature.* Artificial Analysis LLM Leaderboard; METR, Task-Completion Time Horizons. *Short.* The current outer edge of measurable model capability across reasoning, coding, agentic tasks, speed and cost, located through continuously updated comparisons. *Begriff.* Leistungsgrenze aktueller Modelle. *Kurz.* Der aktuelle äußere Rand messbarer Modellleistung in Reasoning, Programmierung, agentischen Aufgaben, Geschwindigkeit und Kosten, bestimmt durch laufend aktualisierte Vergleiche.
- **Asymmetric Amplification.** German Asymmetrische Amplifikation (Deck). The thesis that frontier models strengthen existing human capability unevenly, most strongly where relevant knowledge is externalised, research objects are digitally represented, actions can be executed through software and the environment returns meaningful feedback. It is the overarching thesis organising the capability block of this module. *Source.* CLARIAH and Notes EN, "Frontier LLMs Asymmetrically Amplify Computational Research"; Deck, thesis slide. *Literature.* Pollin 2026, "Asymmetric Amplification" (Digital Humanities Craft). *Short.* The thesis that frontier LLMs strengthen human capability unevenly, chiefly where knowledge is externalised, objects digitally represented, actions run through software and feedback is meaningful. *Begriff.* Asymmetrische Amplifikation. *Kurz.* Die These, dass Frontier-LLMs menschliche Fähigkeiten ungleichmäßig verstärken, vor allem dort, wo Wissen externalisiert ist, Gegenstände digital repräsentiert sind, Handlungen über Software laufen und Feedback aussagekräftig ist.
- **Task-Completion Time Horizon.** German Aufgabenhorizont (proposal). The duration of software tasks that an agent can complete at a specified success probability, used as one capability signal among several and explicitly qualified as no general measure of research capability. *Source.* CLARIAH, "Frontier LLMs Asymmetrically Amplify Computational Research" with footnote 26; Notes EN, same section. *Literature.* Kinniment et al. 2025 (arXiv:2503.14499); METR, Task-Completion Time Horizons. *Short.* The duration of software tasks an agent can complete at a specified success probability, one signal among several and no general measure of research capability. *Begriff.* Aufgabenhorizont. *Kurz.* Die Dauer von Softwareaufgaben, die ein Agent mit einer festgelegten Erfolgswahrscheinlichkeit abschließen kann, ein Fähigkeitssignal unter mehreren und kein allgemeines Maß wissenschaftlicher Leistungsfähigkeit.
- **Jagged Capability (Jagged Alien Intelligence).** German Jagged Alien Intelligence, untranslated in the Deck slide title; no rendering in the Skriptum. The observed capability profile in which a model performs extremely difficult tasks while failing on neighbouring tasks that appear simpler to a human observer, which blocks both naive extrapolation from single successes and dismissal from conspicuous failures. *Source.* CLARIAH and Notes EN, "LLMs as Jagged Alien 'Intelligences'"; Deck. *Literature.* Dell'Acqua et al. 2026 (DOI 10.1287/orsc.2025.21838); Gans 2026 (arXiv:2601.07573); Summerfield 2025. *Short.* A capability profile in which an LLM solves extremely difficult tasks while failing on neighbouring tasks that look simpler to a human observer. *Begriff.* Jagged Alien Intelligence. *Kurz.* Ein Fähigkeitsprofil, in dem ein LLM äußerst schwierige Aufgaben löst und zugleich an benachbarten Aufgaben scheitert, die Menschen einfacher erscheinen.
- **Confabulation.** German Konfabulation (Deck). The production of plausible but unsupported claims, chosen in the scripts as the descriptive term for this behaviour in place of hallucination. The glossary states the substitution, since the adjacent received literature runs under hallucination. *Source.* CLARIAH, "LLMs as Jagged Alien 'Intelligences'", which states the terminological choice explicitly; Deck. *Literature.* No canonical source for the term in this sense; Ji et al. 2023 (DOI 10.1145/3571730) is the standard survey under the received name. *Short.* The production of plausible but unsupported claims, the term the material uses for this behaviour in place of hallucination. *Begriff.* Konfabulation. *Kurz.* Die Erzeugung plausibler, aber nicht gestützter Behauptungen, der Begriff, den das Material für dieses Verhalten anstelle von Halluzination verwendet.
- **Sycophancy.** German Sycophancy, untranslated, with the Deck gloss Tendenz von LLMs, Nutzer:innen zuzustimmen. The tendency to align an answer with a belief the user has expressed even where this reduces factual accuracy, with the methodological consequence that a prompt asking for confirmation of a preferred interpretation is weak evidence. *Source.* CLARIAH and Notes EN, "LLMs as Jagged Alien 'Intelligences'" and the countermeasure in the prompting principles; Deck. *Literature.* Sharma et al. 2023 (arXiv:2310.13548). *Short.* The tendency of an LLM to align an answer with a belief the user has expressed, even where this reduces factual accuracy. *Begriff.* Sycophancy. *Kurz.* Die Tendenz eines LLM, eine Antwort an eine geäußerte Überzeugung der Nutzer:innen anzupassen, auch wenn dies die sachliche Richtigkeit mindert.

### How language models work

- **Large Language Model.** German Large Language Model, used untranslated, with the Skriptum gloss probabilistisches Textsystem. A neural network trained on large tokenised collections to estimate a probability distribution over the next token given the tokens in its context, whose output stays probabilistic across repeated runs of the same input. *Source.* Notes EN, "How Large Language Models Work"; Skriptum DE §2.1. *Literature.* Brown et al. 2020 (arXiv:2005.14165); Vaswani et al. 2017 (arXiv:1706.03762). *Short.* A neural network trained on large tokenised collections to estimate the next-token probability distribution given its context, with probabilistic output across repeated runs. *Begriff.* Large Language Model. *Kurz.* Ein neuronales Netz, das auf großen tokenisierten Sammlungen darauf trainiert ist, eine Wahrscheinlichkeitsverteilung über das nächste Token im gegebenen Kontext zu schätzen, und dessen Ausgabe über wiederholte Läufe probabilistisch bleibt.
- **Next Token Prediction.** German Next Token Prediction, untranslated (Skriptum DE §2.1). The training objective of autoregressive language modelling, which the scripts separate sharply from the capabilities acquired while optimising it, since the objective asks which continuation is probable rather than whether a proposition is true. *Source.* Notes EN, "How Large Language Models Work"; Skriptum DE §2.1 with footnote 1 marking the description as a functional simplification. *Literature.* Brown et al. 2020 (arXiv:2005.14165). *Short.* The training objective of autoregressive language modelling, which asks which continuation is probable and is kept separate from the capabilities acquired while optimising it. *Begriff.* Next Token Prediction. *Kurz.* Das Trainingsziel autoregressiver Sprachmodellierung, das nach der wahrscheinlichen Fortsetzung fragt und von den Fähigkeiten getrennt wird, die bei seiner Optimierung erworben werden.
- **Transformer Architecture and Self-Attention.** German Transformer-Modell (Skriptum DE, footnote 1); Transformer-Architektur is a proposal. The layered architecture in which attention lets information at different positions of the sequence influence one another, so that a token receives a context-dependent representation rather than an isolated one. It carries the bridge to module 3, that a prompt intervenes in the current computation because it changes the sequence the attention operates over. *Source.* Notes EN, "Transformer Architecture"; Deck, context-window slide. *Literature.* Vaswani et al. 2017 (arXiv:1706.03762). *Short.* The layered architecture in which attention lets positions of a sequence influence one another, giving each token a context-dependent representation. *Begriff.* Transformer-Modell. *Kurz.* Die geschichtete Architektur, in der Attention die Positionen einer Sequenz wechselseitig aufeinander wirken lässt, sodass jedes Token eine kontextabhängige Repräsentation erhält.
- **Token and Tokenization.** German Token, Tokenisierung (Skriptum DE §4.3). The discrete units into which a tokenizer converts character sequences before they enter the network, with the consequence that context limits, input cost and output length are measured in tokens rather than in words. *Source.* Notes EN, "Tokenization"; Skriptum DE §4.3 in the path from file to context window. *Literature.* Sennrich, Haddow and Birch 2016 (arXiv:1508.07909). *Short.* The discrete units into which a tokenizer converts character sequences, so that context limits, input cost and output length are measured in tokens. *Begriff.* Token und Tokenisierung. *Kurz.* Die diskreten Einheiten, in die ein Tokenizer Zeichenfolgen umwandelt, sodass Kontextgrenzen, Eingabekosten und Ausgabelänge in Token gemessen werden.
- **Embedding and Contextual Representation.** German Embedding und kontextuelle Repräsentation (proposal); the Skriptum paraphrases as hochdimensionale numerische Repräsentationen (§3.6). The mapping of discrete token identifiers into a high-dimensional vector space, and the repeated transformation of those initial vectors across the network into representations that depend on the surrounding tokens and the current task. It supplies the mechanism behind the claim that wording matters without assuming one fixed meaning per token. *Source.* Notes EN, "Embeddings and Contextual Representations"; Skriptum DE §3.6. *Literature.* Vaswani et al. 2017 (arXiv:1706.03762). *Short.* The mapping of tokens into a high-dimensional vector space and the repeated transformation of these vectors into representations dependent on surrounding tokens and task. *Begriff.* Embedding und kontextuelle Repräsentation. *Kurz.* Die Abbildung von Token-Identifikatoren in einen hochdimensionalen Vektorraum und die wiederholte Transformation dieser Vektoren zu Repräsentationen, die von umgebenden Token und der Aufgabe abhängen.
- **Pretraining.** German Pre-Training (Skriptum DE §2.2). The training stage on large heterogeneous corpora that establishes broad linguistic, conceptual and procedural representations, described as building a repertoire while noting that knowledge and capability stay entangled. *Source.* Notes EN, "Pretraining and Posttraining" and "Training Builds and Shapes the Latent Program Space"; Skriptum DE §2.2; CLARIAH, "Training Shapes the Latent Program Space". *Literature.* Brown et al. 2020 (arXiv:2005.14165). *Short.* The training stage on large heterogeneous corpora that builds broad linguistic, conceptual and procedural representations, in which knowledge and capability remain entangled. *Begriff.* Pre-Training. *Kurz.* Die Trainingsphase auf großen heterogenen Korpora, die breite sprachliche, konzeptuelle und prozedurale Repräsentationen aufbaut und in der Wissen und Fähigkeit verschränkt bleiben.
- **Posttraining.** German Post-Training (Skriptum DE §2.2). The training stages that shape how the learned repertoire is expressed in an assistant, through instruction tuning, demonstrations, preference learning, reinforcement learning and related methods, including interface capabilities such as tool calling. *Source.* Notes EN, "Pretraining and Posttraining"; Skriptum DE §2.2. *Literature.* Ouyang et al. 2022 (arXiv:2203.02155). *Short.* The training stages that shape how the learned repertoire is expressed in an assistant, through instruction tuning, preference learning, reinforcement learning and related methods. *Begriff.* Post-Training. *Kurz.* Die Trainingsphasen, die prägen, wie sich das gelernte Repertoire in einem Assistenten äußert, durch Instruction Tuning, Präferenzlernen, Reinforcement Learning und verwandte Verfahren.
- **Midtraining (Model Spec Midtraining).** German Midtraining (proposal), absent from both German sources. A stage between broad pretraining and later alignment training in which a model is trained on synthetic documents discussing a Model Spec or Constitution, in order to influence how the subsequent alignment training generalises. The scripts mark it as a proposed technique rather than a standardised phase. *Source.* Notes EN, "Training Builds and Shapes the Latent Program Space". *Literature.* Li et al. 2026, "Model Spec Midtraining" (arXiv:2605.02087). *Short.* A proposed stage between pretraining and alignment training that trains a model on synthetic documents about a Model Spec or Constitution, shaping how alignment generalises. *Begriff.* Midtraining. *Kurz.* Eine vorgeschlagene Phase zwischen Pre-Training und Alignment-Training, die ein Modell auf synthetischen Dokumenten über eine Model Spec oder Constitution trainiert, um die Generalisierung des Alignments zu prägen.
- **Parametric Knowledge.** German parametrisches Wissen (proposal); the Skriptum describes the matter in §2.2 without naming it. Knowledge a model can produce because its parameters encode statistical structure from training material, which the scripts contrast with an addressable copy of a training document and therefore with any direct provenance for a generated claim. It grounds the provenance argument of module 5. *Source.* Notes EN, "The Shape of a Wikipedia Article About Zebras" with its three information layers of model parameters, external resources and current context; Skriptum DE §2.2. *Literature.* Petroni et al. 2019 (arXiv:1909.01066), proposed anchor; the scripts use a lossy-compression analogy and mark it explicitly as an analogy. *Short.* Knowledge an LLM can produce because its parameters encode statistical structure from training material, with no addressable copy of training documents and no direct provenance. *Begriff.* parametrisches Wissen. *Kurz.* Wissen, das ein LLM hervorbringen kann, weil seine Parameter statistische Struktur aus Trainingsmaterial kodieren, ohne adressierbare Kopie der Trainingsdokumente und ohne direkte Provenienz.
- **In-Context Learning.** German In-Context Learning (proposal), absent from both German sources. The ability of a model to adapt its behaviour to instructions, examples and information supplied in the current context without any change to model parameters. It is what makes context a working surface at all. *Source.* Notes EN, "Context Engineering Shapes the Model's Working Context" and the training and inference distinction in "Pretraining and Posttraining". *Literature.* Brown et al. 2020 (arXiv:2005.14165); Xie et al. 2022 (arXiv:2111.02080). *Short.* The ability of an LLM to adapt its behaviour to instructions, examples and information in the current context without any change to its parameters. *Begriff.* In-Context Learning. *Kurz.* Die Fähigkeit eines LLM, sein Verhalten an Instruktionen, Beispiele und Informationen im aktuellen Kontext anzupassen, ohne dass sich seine Parameter ändern.

### Latent program space

- **Latent Program Space.** German Latent Program Space, untranslated (Skriptum DE §3.6, marked there as a metaphor). The space of possible learned transformations a model can activate depending on input and context, read in the material as a conceptual model of learned processing routines rather than as a delimited store of classical programs. *Source.* Notes EN, "Prompt Engineering: Finding Coordinates in a Latent Program Space" with the five-item terminology list; CLARIAH, "Prompt Engineering as Search in a Latent Program Space"; Skriptum DE §3.6 with figure 7. *Literature.* Chollet 2019 (arXiv:1911.01547); Bonnet and Macfarlane 2024 (arXiv:2411.08706); Chollet 2023, "How I Think About LLM Prompt Engineering". *Short.* The space of learned transformations an LLM can activate depending on input and context, read as a conceptual model of learned processing routines. *Begriff.* Latent Program Space. *Kurz.* Der Raum gelernter Transformationen, die ein LLM je nach Eingabe und Kontext aktivieren kann, verstanden als konzeptuelles Modell gelernter Verarbeitungsroutinen.
- **Vector Program.** German Vektorprogramm (proposal); the Skriptum paraphrases as gelernte Verarbeitungsroutinen (§3.6). A learned and distributed transformation across high-dimensional representations that realises one capability or behaviour, distinguished explicitly from a discrete symbolic program stored inside the model. *Source.* Notes EN and CLARIAH, five-item terminology list; Skriptum DE §3.6. *Literature.* Chollet 2023. *Short.* A learned, distributed transformation across high-dimensional representations that realises one capability or behaviour, distinct from a discrete symbolic program stored inside the model. *Begriff.* Vektorprogramm. *Kurz.* Eine gelernte, verteilte Transformation über hochdimensionale Repräsentationen, die eine Fähigkeit oder ein Verhalten realisiert und sich von einem im Modell gespeicherten diskreten symbolischen Programm unterscheidet.
- **Program Query.** German Programmabfrage (proposal), absent from both German sources. The prompt considered as an address or search signal into the learned repertoire, where one part of a prompt steers towards a region of that repertoire and another supplies the material the selected transformation operates on. *Source.* Notes EN and CLARIAH, five-item terminology list. *Literature.* Chollet 2023; Xie et al. 2022 (arXiv:2111.02080); Reynolds and McDonell 2021 (DOI 10.1145/3411763.3451760). *Editorial decision 2026-08-20.* Program Query is canonical. Both canonical scripts carry it verbatim in their terminology lists, while Program Key appears in no text of the teaching line and was a coinage of the earlier terminology matrix; it is recorded here as a superseded variant so that older notes remain resolvable. *Short.* The prompt as an address into the learned repertoire, where one part steers towards a region of it and another supplies the material to process. *Begriff.* Programmabfrage. *Kurz.* Der Prompt als Adresse in das gelernte Repertoire, wobei ein Teil auf eine Region dieses Repertoires lenkt und ein anderer das Material liefert, auf dem die gewählte Transformation operiert.
- **Reasoning and Test-Time Compute.** German Reasoning, on the Deck as Thinking Token; the Skriptum names Reasoning in passing (§3.5). The allocation of additional computation during inference before a final answer is committed, read as a partial shift of the search burden from external prompt variation into a single model invocation. It is the fifth item of the latent-program-space terminology list. *Source.* CLARIAH, "Reasoning and Test-Time Compute as Internal Search"; Notes EN, terminology list and the principle "Use Reasoning Selectively". *Literature.* Snell et al. 2024 (arXiv:2408.03314). *Short.* The allocation of additional computation during inference before a final answer, shifting part of the search burden from external prompt variation into one model invocation. *Begriff.* Reasoning. *Kurz.* Die Zuweisung zusätzlicher Rechenleistung während der Inferenz vor einer finalen Antwort, die einen Teil der Suchlast von externer Promptvariation in einen einzelnen Modellaufruf verlagert.
- **Mechanistic Interpretability.** German Mechanistische Interpretierbarkeit (Skriptum DE §3.6, as Interpretierbarkeitsverfahren). Research into the internal computations through which a model produces behaviour, used in the material for the limited claim that model computation is structured and input-dependent. It supplies no empirical map of a latent program space. *Source.* CLARIAH, "Excursus: Mechanistic Interpretability"; Notes EN, "Excursus: Mechanistic Interpretability and Activation Paths"; Skriptum DE §3.6 with figures 7 and 8. *Literature.* Lindsey et al. 2025 (Transformer Circuits); Sofroniew et al. 2026 (Transformer Circuits). *Short.* Research into the internal computations through which an LLM produces behaviour, invoked only for the claim that model computation is structured and input-dependent. *Begriff.* Mechanistische Interpretierbarkeit. *Kurz.* Forschung zu den internen Berechnungen, durch die ein LLM Verhalten hervorbringt, hier nur für die Aussage herangezogen, dass Modellberechnung strukturiert und eingabeabhängig ist.

### Assistant layer

- **Assistant Character.** German Assistentenfigur (Skriptum DE §2.2). The conversational persona a provider shapes and stabilises through training, runtime instructions, policy layers and product design, kept separate from the underlying model and from any claim about subjective experience. *Source.* CLARIAH and Notes EN, "The Assistant Is a Character Generated by the Model"; Skriptum DE §2.2 with figure 3. *Literature.* Anthropic, "Claude's Character"; Anthropic 2026, "Claude's Constitution". *Short.* The conversational persona a provider shapes and stabilises through training, runtime instructions, policy layers and product design, separate from the underlying model. *Begriff.* Assistentenfigur. *Kurz.* Die Gesprächspersona, die ein Anbieter durch Training, Laufzeitinstruktionen, Richtlinienebenen und Produktgestaltung formt und stabilisiert, getrennt vom zugrunde liegenden Modell.
- **Constitution, Character Training and System Prompt.** German Systeminstruktionen (Skriptum DE §2.2); Constitution untranslated in footnote 2. Three layers the scripts insist on separating, a training artefact such as a Constitution or Model Spec that shapes what is learned, character training and post-training that stabilise behavioural dispositions, and a system prompt that operates at runtime inside one deployment. *Source.* Notes EN, "How Anthropic Shapes Claude's Assistant Character"; Skriptum DE §2.2 with footnote 2 marking the ontological reading as contested. *Literature.* Anthropic 2026, "Claude's New Constitution"; Anthropic, "System Prompts". *Short.* Three separate layers, a training artefact such as a Constitution, character training that stabilises dispositions, and a system prompt operating at runtime within one deployment. *Begriff.* Constitution, Character Training und Systeminstruktionen. *Kurz.* Drei getrennte Ebenen, ein Trainingsartefakt wie eine Constitution, Character Training zur Stabilisierung von Verhaltensdispositionen und Systeminstruktionen, die zur Laufzeit innerhalb eines Deployments wirken.

### Multimodality

- **Vision-Language Model.** German Vision-Language-Modell (proposal); the Skriptum speaks of multimodaler Verarbeitung (§4.3). A model that processes visual and linguistic information within the same task, so that a general-purpose multimodal system can transcribe a facsimile from image and instruction without being a dedicated OCR or HTR system. *Source.* CLARIAH, "Multimodality and Vision-Language Models"; Notes EN, "Multimodality & Vision Language Models". *Literature.* Gemini Team 2023 (arXiv:2312.11805). *Short.* A model processing visual and linguistic information within one task, so it can transcribe a facsimile without being a dedicated OCR or HTR system. *Begriff.* Vision-Language-Modell. *Kurz.* Ein Modell, das visuelle und sprachliche Information in derselben Aufgabe verarbeitet und dadurch ein Faksimile transkribieren kann, ohne ein dediziertes OCR- oder HTR-System zu sein.
- **Contextually Plausible Reading.** German kontextuell plausible Lesung (proposal); the Skriptum describes the failure in §2.1 without naming it. The characteristic failure mode of transcription by a general multimodal model, where the generated reading fits the linguistic context while the visual evidence of the source supports a different one, so that fluency and internal coherence carry no information about source fidelity. It is a coinage of the teaching line. *Source.* CLARIAH, "Example Result and Critical Review" and "Multimodality and Vision-Language Models"; Notes EN, "Hands on 1 (Example Result)". *Literature.* No canonical source. *Short.* A transcription failure of multimodal LLMs in which the reading fits the linguistic context while the visual evidence of the source supports a different one. *Begriff.* kontextuell plausible Lesung. *Kurz.* Ein Transkriptionsfehler multimodaler LLMs, bei dem die Lesung zum sprachlichen Kontext passt, während die visuelle Evidenz der Quelle eine andere stützt.
- **Emergent Capability.** German Emergenz (Deck, in the open question on zero-shot transcription). A capability that appears without having been trained for separately, used in the scripts as an open question about zero-shot handwriting recognition rather than as an established classification, because model scale and training composition are not observable for proprietary systems. *Source.* CLARIAH, "Example Result and Critical Review", closing question; Notes EN, "Multimodality & Vision Language Models". *Literature.* Wei et al. 2022 (arXiv:2206.07682) and the counterposition in Schaeffer, Miranda and Koyejo 2023 (arXiv:2304.15004), both proposed anchors that the scripts do not cite. *Short.* A capability appearing without separate training, an open question for zero-shot handwriting recognition since proprietary systems do not disclose scale and training composition. *Begriff.* Emergenz. *Kurz.* Eine Fähigkeit, die ohne gesondertes Training auftritt, hier eine offene Frage für die Zero-Shot-Handschriftenerkennung, da proprietäre Systeme Modellgröße und Trainingszusammensetzung nicht offenlegen.

## 2 Prompt Engineering

- **Prompt Engineering.** German Prompt Engineering, untranslated (Skriptum DE, chapter 3). The iterative development of individual prompts by changing their content, their structure or the prompting techniques applied to them. The material defines the term deliberately more narrowly than context engineering, since it addresses one input sequence rather than the whole information environment of a longer working trajectory, and it replaces neither missing domain knowledge nor missing project context. *Source.* Skriptum DE §3.1 with figure 4; Notes EN, "Prompting Strategies"; CLARIAH, "Prompt Engineering". *Literature.* Schulhoff et al. 2024 (arXiv:2406.06608); Liu et al. 2023 (DOI 10.1145/3560815); Reynolds and McDonell 2021 (DOI 10.1145/3411763.3451760); Anthropic, Prompt engineering overview. *Short.* The iterative development of individual prompts by changing their content, structure or techniques, addressing one input sequence and therefore narrower than context engineering. *Begriff.* Prompt Engineering. *Kurz.* Die iterative Entwicklung einzelner Prompts durch Änderung von Inhalt, Struktur oder Techniken, bezogen auf eine Eingabesequenz und damit enger gefasst als Context Engineering.
- **Prompt.** German Prompt, with the Skriptum gloss die für einen Modellaufruf bereitgestellte Eingabesequenz (§3.1). The input sequence supplied for one model call, which may contain a task, source material, context, requirements, constraints, examples, procedural instructions and specifications for the expected output. *Source.* Skriptum DE §3.1; CLARIAH, "Prompt Engineering". *Literature.* Schulhoff et al. 2024 (arXiv:2406.06608). *Short.* The input sequence supplied for one model call, which may contain task, source material, context, requirements, constraints, examples, procedural instructions and output specifications. *Begriff.* Prompt. *Kurz.* Die für einen Modellaufruf bereitgestellte Eingabesequenz, die Aufgabe, Quellenmaterial, Kontext, Anforderungen, Einschränkungen, Beispiele, Vorgehensanweisungen und Vorgaben zur Ausgabe enthalten kann.
- **Bounded Specification.** German Begrenzte Spezifikation (Skriptum DE §3.2). A prompt understood as a specification of a current task inside an already existing knowledge and working context, which points at persistent project rules instead of restating them and therefore stays compact while remaining precise. It is the hinge between module 2 and module 3, since the prompt can be bounded only where a maintained knowledge base exists to point at. *Source.* Skriptum DE §3.2 with the TEI example and §3.8; Notes EN, "Transcribe a Facsimile with Gemini 3.7 Flash". *Literature.* In-house formulation of the teaching line. *Short.* A prompt specifying a current task inside an existing knowledge and working context, pointing at persistent project rules so that it stays compact and precise. *Begriff.* Begrenzte Spezifikation. *Kurz.* Ein Prompt, der eine aktuelle Aufgabe innerhalb eines bestehenden Wissens- und Arbeitskontextes spezifiziert und auf persistente Projektregeln verweist, sodass er kompakt und präzise bleibt.
- **Task, Context, Rules, Output.** German list of seven parts in the Skriptum, Ziel, Ausgangslage, Anforderungen, Einschränkungen, Vorgehen, Ausgabeform, Abschlusskriterium (§3.2). The four-part working structure for research prompts, separating what the model should do, which source or project information it needs, which scholarly and technical constraints govern the result, and in which form the result is returned, so that the parts can be revised independently. The English texts teach four parts and the Skriptum seven; both lists are instance-dependent renderings of the same separation, and the glossary states neither as exclusive. *Source.* CLARIAH, "Prompt Engineering" and the two transcription prompts; Notes EN, "Prompting Strategies", item Structure the Prompt; Skriptum DE §3.2. *Literature.* Schulhoff et al. 2024 (arXiv:2406.06608); Google, Prompt Design Strategies. *Short.* A prompt structure separating task, needed source or project information, governing constraints and output form, so that each part can be revised independently. *Begriff.* Task, Context, Rules, Output. *Kurz.* Eine Gliederung von Prompts, die Aufgabe, benötigte Quellen- oder Projektinformation, maßgebliche Einschränkungen und Ausgabeform trennt, sodass jeder Teil unabhängig überarbeitet werden kann.
- **Role Prompting.** German Role Prompting (Skriptum DE §3.3). A brief functional assignment of a role to the model, which can influence terminology, style, perspective and level of detail while adding no factual knowledge, so that it does not reliably improve accuracy or reasoning. *Source.* Skriptum DE §3.3; CLARIAH, "Prompting Is Weird"; Notes EN, "Prompting Is Weird, and Keeps Changing". *Literature.* Basil et al. 2025 (arXiv:2512.05858); Hu and Collier 2024 (arXiv:2402.10811). *Short.* A brief functional assignment of a role to an LLM, which can influence terminology, style, perspective and detail while adding no factual knowledge. *Begriff.* Role Prompting. *Kurz.* Eine knappe funktionale Rollenzuweisung an ein LLM, die Terminologie, Stil, Perspektive und Detailgrad beeinflussen kann, ohne Faktenwissen hinzuzufügen.
- **Persona Prompting (Persona Engineering).** German Persona Prompting (Skriptum DE §3.3); Persona Engineering on the Deck slide. A worked-out perspective with background, experience, goals, constraints and usage situation, used as a structured viewpoint for reviewing an artefact. Its outputs are hypotheses about users and carry no empirical user data. The Skriptum keeps role and persona prompting distinct and warns against equating them. *Source.* Skriptum DE §3.3 with the workshop-participant persona; Notes EN, "Persona Engineering"; Deck. *Literature.* Hu and Collier 2024 (arXiv:2402.10811). *Short.* A worked-out perspective with background, experience, goals, constraints and usage situation, used to review an artefact and yielding hypotheses about users without empirical user data. *Begriff.* Persona Prompting. *Kurz.* Eine ausgearbeitete Perspektive mit Hintergrund, Erfahrung, Zielen, Einschränkungen und Nutzungssituation, die zur Prüfung eines Artefakts dient und Hypothesen über Nutzer:innen ohne empirische Nutzerdaten liefert.
- **Self-Revision.** German Self-Revision (Skriptum DE §3.4). A staged interaction in which the model first produces a draft, then checks it against explicit criteria, then revises only the confirmed findings. This surfaces errors while constituting no independent verification, since the same model can miss or rationalise its own assumptions. *Source.* Skriptum DE §3.4; Deck, hands-on step 3; Notes EN, "Generate, Compare and Reconcile". *Literature.* Zheng et al. 2023 (arXiv:2306.05685), proposed anchor for the self-enhancement limit. *Short.* A staged interaction in which an LLM drafts, checks the draft against explicit criteria and revises only confirmed findings, surfacing errors without constituting independent verification. *Begriff.* Self-Revision. *Kurz.* Eine gestufte Interaktion, in der ein LLM einen Entwurf erstellt, ihn gegen explizite Kriterien prüft und nur bestätigte Befunde überarbeitet, was Fehler sichtbar macht, ohne eine unabhängige Verifikation darzustellen.
- **Structured Output.** German Strukturierte Ausgaben (Skriptum DE §3.4). An output constrained to a defined format such as JSON, XML, a Markdown table or a file structure, which reduces ambiguity and enables deterministic checks. The material separates four levels of correctness, syntactic conformity, structural completeness, semantic correctness and scholarly adequacy, and states that the first two prove nothing about the last. *Source.* Skriptum DE §3.4; CLARIAH, "From Generated Text to Structured Data"; Notes EN, ".txt → JSON → CSV". *Literature.* The four levels are a distinction of the material. *Short.* Output constrained to a defined format such as JSON or XML, enabling deterministic checks of syntax and structure that prove nothing about scholarly adequacy. *Begriff.* Strukturierte Ausgaben. *Kurz.* Eine auf ein definiertes Format wie JSON oder XML beschränkte Ausgabe, die deterministische Prüfungen von Syntax und Struktur ermöglicht, welche nichts über wissenschaftliche Angemessenheit beweisen.
- **Prompt Sensitivity.** German Promptwirkungen, with the qualifier modell-, aufgaben- und sprachabhängig (Skriptum DE §3.5). The empirical finding that model output can change in response to linguistic details with little relation to the task, so that prompting effects are local to model, version, task, language, position, metric and random variation and are treated as experimental interventions rather than as transferable rules. *Source.* Skriptum DE §3.5 with figures 5 and 6 and the seven-step evaluation procedure; CLARIAH and Notes EN, "Prompting Is Weird". *Literature.* Li et al. 2023 (arXiv:2307.11760); Yin et al. 2024 (arXiv:2402.14531); Battle and Gollapudi 2024 (arXiv:2402.10949); Rajeev et al. 2025 (arXiv:2503.01781). *Short.* The finding that LLM output can shift with linguistic details of little task relevance, so prompting effects are local and treated as experimental interventions. *Begriff.* Promptwirkungen. *Kurz.* Der Befund, dass sich LLM-Ausgaben mit sprachlichen Details von geringer Aufgabenrelevanz verändern können, sodass Promptwirkungen lokal sind und als experimentelle Interventionen behandelt werden.
- **Clarifying Questions.** German Rückfragen (UFL, "Begriffe im Workshop"). A prompting strategy that asks the model to put a small, capped number of questions about goal, role and context before it acts, so that the context a task needs is built up in a question-and-answer exchange. The UFL prompts combine it with an explicit stop, asking for the questions first and for implementation only after the answers, and they let the model revise once after the answers arrive. *Source.* UFL, Hands-on 1 prompt with its analysis in "Prompt Engineering ist so 2025!"; Hands-on 2, first step; Hands-on 4, first prompt. *Literature.* No canonical source read for this entry. *Short.* A prompting strategy in which the model first asks a few questions about goal and context before it starts working. *Begriff.* Rückfragen. *Kurz.* Prompting-Strategie, bei der das Modell vor der Arbeit einige Fragen zu Ziel und Kontext stellt.
- **Milestone.** German Meilenstein (UFL, Hands-on 1). A bounded intermediate result at which an agentic run stops until a person has checked and released it, requested in the prompt by asking the agent to propose milestones and to halt after each one. Milestones steer the process of a longer run and keep each step reviewable, and the UFL notes pair them with one version-control commit per task and milestone. The Promptotyping specification describes the same device as a verification milestone, a defined checkpoint at which the work is verified with deterministic tools and validated by expert judgement before it continues. *Source.* UFL, Hands-on 1 prompt with its analysis in "Prompt Engineering ist so 2025!"; "Hands-on 1: Follow-Up Prompts zu Refactoring und Kuratieren"; Hands-on 4, second prompt; "Fancy Research Tools", step 3. *Literature.* DHCraft, Promptotyping specification, glossary entry Verification Milestone. *Short.* A checkpoint at which an AI agent stops until a person has checked and released the intermediate result. *Begriff.* Meilenstein. *Kurz.* Zwischenstand, an dem der Agent anhält, bis ein Mensch das Ergebnis geprüft und freigegeben hat.

## 3 Knowledge and Context Engineering

### Context engineering

- **Context Engineering.** German Context Engineering, untranslated (Skriptum DE, chapter 4). The systematic selection, organisation, maintenance and provision of the task-specific information state for a model call or an agentic run. It determines which information a model receives, in which form and order it arrives, when further information is loaded, and what deliberately stays outside the current context. *Source.* Skriptum DE §4.1; CLARIAH, "Context Engineering Shapes the Working Context"; Notes EN, "Context Engineering Shapes the Model's Working Context". *Literature.* Mei et al. 2025, cited by Skriptum DE §4.1; Karpathy 2025, coinage post; Anthropic 2025, "Effective context engineering for AI agents"; LangChain 2025, four-part strategy taxonomy. *Short.* The systematic selection, organisation, maintenance and provision of the task-specific information state for a model call or an agentic run. *Begriff.* Context Engineering. *Kurz.* Die systematische Auswahl, Organisation, Pflege und Bereitstellung des aufgabenspezifischen Informationsstands für einen Modellaufruf oder einen agentischen Lauf.
- **Context Window.** German Context Window (Skriptum DE §4.2); Kontextfenster (Deck). The finite token-based processing space of one model call, containing the supplied input and those generated tokens that remain in the active sequence. Its nominal size is a technical limit and no target for how much information to supply. *Source.* Skriptum DE §4.2; CLARIAH, "Context Engineering Shapes the Working Context"; Notes EN, "Context Window, Context Rot and Distillation"; Deck. *Literature.* Vaswani et al. 2017 (arXiv:1706.03762). *Short.* The finite token-based processing space of one model call, whose nominal size is a technical limit that sets no target for the amount of input. *Begriff.* Context Window. *Kurz.* Der endliche, tokenbasierte Verarbeitungsraum eines Modellaufrufs, dessen nominale Größe eine technische Grenze ist, die kein Ziel für den Umfang der Eingabe vorgibt.
- **Working Context.** German Working Context, untranslated (Skriptum DE §4.5 and §4.6). The task-specific configuration of information, documents, data access, instructions, tool descriptions, permissions, current state and feedback assembled for one assignment. Not every component is serialised into tokens, since an agent may reach a dataset through a tool while only selected results enter the model context. *Source.* Skriptum DE §4.5 with the worked example for one edition page and §4.6 with the four-way loading matrix; Notes EN, "Project Knowledge Base and Working Context"; Deck. *Literature.* Pollin, *Promptotyping* (monograph, review draft 0.9, 2026). *Short.* The task-specific configuration of information, documents, data access, instructions, tool descriptions, permissions, state and feedback assembled for one assignment, not all of which becomes tokens. *Begriff.* Working Context. *Kurz.* Die aufgabenspezifische Konfiguration aus Informationen, Dokumenten, Datenzugriffen, Instruktionen, Werkzeugbeschreibungen, Berechtigungen, Zustand und Feedback für einen Auftrag, von der nicht alles als Token in den Modellkontext eingeht.
- **Context Rot.** German Context Rot, untranslated (Skriptum DE §4.2; Deck). Observed degradation in a model's ability to retrieve and apply relevant information as the active context becomes longer, denser or more distracting. The scripts refuse to reduce it to one causal mechanism and name position effects, distractors, conflicting information and obsolete intermediate state as contributors. *Source.* Skriptum DE §4.2; CLARIAH, "Context Engineering Shapes the Working Context"; Notes EN, "Context Rot"; Deck, with the caveat that the reported evidence is task-specific. *Literature.* Hong, Troynikov and Huber 2025, *Context Rot* (Chroma). *Short.* Observed degradation in an LLM's ability to retrieve and apply relevant information as the active context grows longer, denser or more distracting. *Begriff.* Context Rot. *Kurz.* Beobachtete Verschlechterung der Fähigkeit eines LLM, relevante Information abzurufen und anzuwenden, wenn der aktive Kontext länger, dichter oder ablenkender wird.
- **Dense and Sufficient Context.** German Dichter und hinreichender Kontext (Skriptum DE §4.2; Deck). The stated target quantity for a working context, as bounded as possible while carrying every distinction the task requires. The scripts argue against a shortening rule, because radical reduction removes provenance, conditions and uncertainty. *Source.* Skriptum DE §4.2, closing paragraph; Notes EN, "Why AI Agents Need Context"; Deck. *Literature.* Formulation of the teaching line derived from the Context Rot finding. *Short.* The target for a working context, as bounded as possible while carrying every distinction the task requires, including provenance, conditions and uncertainty. *Begriff.* Dichter und hinreichender Kontext. *Kurz.* Die Zielgröße für einen Working Context, so begrenzt wie möglich und zugleich Träger jeder Unterscheidung, die die Aufgabe verlangt, einschließlich Provenienz, Bedingungen und Unsicherheit.
- **Context Compression.** German Context Compression, untranslated (Skriptum DE §4.4; Deck). Reduction of the amount of information entering an active context, through selection of relevant passages, summary, removal of repetition, aggregation of data, retention of representative examples or compaction of the previous working history. *Source.* Skriptum DE §4.4; Notes EN, "Selecting, Structuring and Distilling Knowledge"; Deck. *Literature.* Pollin, *Promptotyping* (monograph, review draft 0.9, 2026). *Short.* Reduction of the information entering an active context through selecting relevant passages, summarising, removing repetition, aggregating data, keeping representative examples or compacting working history. *Begriff.* Context Compression. *Kurz.* Verringerung der Information, die in einen aktiven Kontext eingeht, durch Auswahl relevanter Passagen, Zusammenfassung, Entfernung von Wiederholungen, Aggregation von Daten, Beibehaltung repräsentativer Beispiele oder Verdichtung des bisherigen Arbeitsverlaufs.
- **Distillation.** German Distillation, untranslated (Skriptum DE §4.4 and §7.4; Deck). The stronger operation beyond compression, transforming an existing understanding into a selective, structured and inspectable representation that preserves the concepts, relations, conditions, uncertainties and rationales a given purpose requires. The same material is distilled differently for a general introduction, an implementation task and a verification task. *Source.* Skriptum DE §4.4 with its three operations of selection, structuring and condensation, and §7.4 as a Promptotyping phase; Notes EN, "Selecting, Structuring and Distilling Knowledge"; Deck. *Literature.* Pollin, *Promptotyping* (monograph, review draft 0.9, 2026). *Editorial decision 2026-08-20.* The sense used here is canonical inside this teaching line and is disambiguated explicitly against the established machine-learning sense of knowledge distillation, where a smaller model is trained on the outputs of a larger one. The sense used here operates on documents and understanding, the machine-learning sense on model parameters, and the audience meets both. *Short.* Transforming an understanding into a selective, structured and inspectable representation preserving what a given purpose requires, operating on documents, unlike knowledge distillation in machine learning. *Begriff.* Distillation. *Kurz.* Die Überführung eines Verständnisses in eine selektive, strukturierte und prüfbare Repräsentation, die bewahrt, was ein Zweck verlangt, und anders als Knowledge Distillation im maschinellen Lernen auf Dokumenten operiert.
- **Over-Distillation.** German Überdestillation (Skriptum DE §4.4). The counter-risk of distillation, present when provenance, uncertainty or details needed for action are lost, so that a shorter document turns several alternative statements into one apparently unambiguous rule. *Source.* Skriptum DE §4.4. *Literature.* Term of the teaching line. *Short.* The counter-risk of distillation, in which provenance, uncertainty or actionable detail is lost and several alternative statements collapse into one apparently unambiguous rule. *Begriff.* Überdestillation. *Kurz.* Das Gegenrisiko der Distillation, bei dem Provenienz, Unsicherheit oder handlungsrelevante Details verloren gehen und mehrere alternative Aussagen zu einer scheinbar eindeutigen Regel verschmelzen.
- **Agent Memory (Auto Memory).** German Memory, untranslated (UFL, "Memory"). Notes that an agent derives from its own work and reloads in later sessions, such as preferences, corrections, ongoing work and pointers to sources. The agent writes them itself, which separates memory from an instruction file that people write. Claude Code keeps such notes per repository in a local directory with an index whose opening portion enters every session and has the feature on by default; Codex turns finished chats into memory files in the background and has the feature off by default. Both vendors treat memory as context and as a recall aid, so binding rules belong in an instruction file, and an action that must be blocked needs a hook. *Source.* UFL, "Memory" with the comparison of Claude Code and Codex, and "Context Engineering", where memory appears under managing context. *Literature.* Anthropic, "How Claude Remembers Your Project"; OpenAI, "Memories" (Codex documentation). *Short.* Notes an AI agent writes itself from its work and reloads in later sessions, serving as a recall aid without binding force. *Begriff.* Memory. *Kurz.* Notizen, die der Agent selbst aus der Arbeit ableitet und in späteren Sessions wieder lädt, als Erinnerungshilfe ohne verbindliche Regeln.

### Knowledge engineering

- **Knowledge Engineering.** German Knowledge Engineering, untranslated (Skriptum DE, chapter 5). The construction and maintenance of explicit, inspectable and revisable project knowledge, which makes the relevant concepts, rules, decisions, constraints and uncertainties visible enough to be read, criticised and continued. Its aim is purpose-bound rather than a complete representation of all available information. *Source.* Skriptum DE §5.1; Notes EN, "Why Knowledge Must Be Made Explicit"; CLARIAH, knowledge-engineering section. *Literature.* Studer, Benjamins and Fensel 1998 (DOI 10.1016/S0169-023X(97)00056-6); Newell 1982 (DOI 10.1016/0004-3702(82)90012-1); Schreiber et al. 2000, CommonKADS. *Short.* The purpose-bound construction and maintenance of explicit, inspectable, revisable project knowledge, making concepts, rules, decisions, constraints and uncertainties visible enough for critique and continuation. *Begriff.* Knowledge Engineering. *Kurz.* Der zweckgebundene Aufbau und die Pflege expliziten, prüfbaren und revidierbaren Projektwissens, das Begriffe, Regeln, Entscheidungen, Einschränkungen und Unsicherheiten so sichtbar macht, dass sie kritisiert und fortgeführt werden können.
- **Knowledge Acquisition.** German Knowledge Acquisition, untranslated (Skriptum DE §5.2); Wissenserwerb on the Deck. The elicitation and explicitation of relevant knowledge from two sources, existing documents and data on one side, implicit knowledge of people and organisations on the other, through document analysis, interviews, workshops, observation of workflows, error analysis and joint modelling sessions. *Source.* Skriptum DE §5.2 with the `<supplied>` example of an unwritten but practised rule; Notes EN, "Scholar Centred Design and Requirements Engineering"; Deck. *Literature.* Studer, Benjamins and Fensel 1998; Schreiber et al. 2000, CommonKADS. *Short.* The elicitation and explicitation of knowledge from documents and data and from the implicit knowledge of people and organisations, through analysis, interviews, workshops and observation. *Begriff.* Knowledge Acquisition. *Kurz.* Die Erhebung und Explizierung von Wissen aus Dokumenten und Daten sowie aus dem impliziten Wissen von Personen und Organisationen, durch Analyse, Interviews, Workshops und Beobachtung.
- **Tacit Knowledge and Externalisation.** German Implizites Wissen (Skriptum DE §5.1 and §5.2); the Deck uses the slide title "I know things". Knowledge held by people, teams or institutions without being documented in a form another person or system can inspect. The material locates the starting point of knowledge engineering in the gap between knowledge possessed and knowledge operationally available. *Source.* Notes EN, "'I Know Things'" and "Why Knowledge Must Be Made Explicit"; Skriptum DE §5.1; Deck. *Literature.* Nonaka 1994 (DOI 10.1287/orsc.5.1.14). *Short.* Knowledge held by people, teams or institutions without inspectable documentation, whose gap to operationally available knowledge is where knowledge engineering starts. *Begriff.* Implizites Wissen. *Kurz.* Wissen, das Personen, Teams oder Institutionen ohne prüfbare Dokumentation besitzen und dessen Abstand zum operativ verfügbaren Wissen den Ausgangspunkt des Knowledge Engineering bildet.
- **Knowledge Modelling.** German Wissensmodellierung (Deck). The classical construction of a knowledge base by identifying the concepts of a domain, representing them formally and making them queryable, named as one of three neighbouring traditions that AI-supported knowledge work draws on. The material states the shift from the classical form explicitly, that the formalisation target is structured natural language with light metadata rather than logic and ontology, because the language model supplies the language understanding. *Source.* Notes EN, "Knowledge Modelling, Personal Information Management and Project Management"; Deck, three-tradition slide. *Literature.* Russell and Norvig 2020; Allen et al. 2023 (DOI 10.4230/TGDK.1.1.3). *Short.* The classical construction of a knowledge base by identifying, formalising and querying domain concepts, shifted in the material towards structured natural language with light metadata. *Begriff.* Wissensmodellierung. *Kurz.* Der klassische Aufbau einer Wissensbasis durch Identifikation, Formalisierung und Abfrage von Domänenbegriffen, den das Material hin zu strukturierter natürlicher Sprache mit leichten Metadaten verschiebt.
- **Personal Information Management.** German Personal Information Management, untranslated (Deck). The study of how people acquire, organise, maintain, retrieve, use and share information across formats and locations, with fragmentation as its core problem and the personal information collection as its core concept. *Source.* Notes EN, "Knowledge Modelling, Personal Information Management and Project Management"; Deck. *Literature.* Jones, Dinneen, Capra, Diekema and Pérez-Quiñones 2017 (DOI 10.1081/E-ELIS4-120053695). *Short.* The study of how people acquire, organise, maintain, retrieve, use and share information across formats and locations, with fragmentation as its core problem. *Begriff.* Personal Information Management. *Kurz.* Die Untersuchung, wie Menschen Information über Formate und Orte hinweg erwerben, organisieren, pflegen, wiederfinden, nutzen und teilen, mit Fragmentierung als Kernproblem.
- **Project Knowledge Base.** German Project Knowledge Base, untranslated (Skriptum DE §4.5 and §5.3), with the descriptive gloss Wissensbasis. - **Knowledge-Base Governance and Curation.** German Governance und Kuration (Skriptum DE §5.7). Governance sets the rules for building, changing and using a knowledge base; curation applies those rules to the actual holdings, structurally for names, metadata, links, document types, versions and duplicates, and substantively for contradictions, outdated rules, missing constraints and over-condensed passages. An agent may locate problems and propose changes while substantive changes require human responsibility. The UFL notes render curation as Kuratieren and separate structural curation of frontmatter, tags, links and maps of content, whose checks are unambiguous and whose changes are easy to reverse, from content curation of wording, duplicates, contradictions and outdated statements, whose checks are judgments and whose changes are hard to reverse. - **Docling.** German Docling (UFL, "Docling: Vom PDF zum Wissensdokument"). The UFL notes use it to make the acquisition step of knowledge modelling concrete. - **Vault (Obsidian Vault).** German Vault, Obsidian-Vault (UFL, "Obsidian"). A folder on the local file system, including its subfolders, whose notes Obsidian stores as Markdown plain-text files, so that the data stay on the person's own computer in an open format that other editors can read. The UFL notes describe three levels on which a vault can work, personal information in the sense of personal information management, project and work, and knowledge structure, which ranges from informal structuring through links, tags and maps of content to formal modelling with document types, relations and schemata. In Hands-on 3 the project knowledge base becomes a vault by being opened in Obsidian, and person and agent then work on the same files. *Source.* UFL, "Obsidian", "Das Wissensdokument im Vault" and "Hands-on 3: Die Wissensbasis wird ein Obsidian-Vault". *Literature.* Obsidian Help, "How Obsidian stores data". *Short.* A local folder of Markdown files that Obsidian opens as a linked knowledge collection, readable by people and AI agents alike. *Begriff.* Vault. *Kurz.* Lokaler Ordner mit Markdown-Dateien, den Obsidian als verknüpfte Wissenssammlung öffnet und den Menschen und AI Agents gleichermaßen lesen.
- **Tag and Tag Taxonomy.** German Tag, Tag-Taxonomie (UFL, "Tags und Tag-Taxonomie"). A tag is a keyword attribute, set inline or in the `tags` property, that cuts across folders and can be combined with others; links connect single concepts, while tags name broad categories. A tag taxonomy is a controlled vocabulary of non-overlapping categories inside which new tags arise, and it prevents the synonyms, misspellings and uneven granularity that uncontrolled tagging produces. The UFL example uses the three categories topic, function and source, keeps a document's maturity in its `status` field and records the vocabulary in a `TAG-TAXONOMY.md` against which tags are checked during curation. *Source.* UFL, "Tags und Tag-Taxonomie", Hands-on 3 and "Kuratieren: Struktur und Inhalt". *Literature.* Obsidian Help, "Tags". *Short.* A controlled vocabulary of non-overlapping tag categories, within which new tags may arise, keeping tagging consistent across a vault. *Begriff.* Tag und Tag-Taxonomie. *Kurz.* Kontrolliertes Vokabular aus sich nicht überlappenden Tag-Kategorien, innerhalb derer neue Tags entstehen, damit die Verschlagwortung einheitlich bleibt.
- **Map of Content (MOC).** German Map of Content (MOC), untranslated (UFL, "Maps of Content"). A curated entry note into a subject area that gathers links to related documents, each with a one-line explanation. MOCs can be nested, from a `HOME.md` at the root through topic MOCs to sub-MOCs or knowledge documents, and they lie over the documents without changing them or imposing a strict hierarchy. For agents a MOC is navigation infrastructure, since the context window cannot hold a larger vault, so the agent starts at an entry point and follows the links. *Source.* UFL, "Maps of Content" and "Vault-Governance". *Literature.* Milo, "MOCs Overview" (Linking Your Thinking), for the received notion of a map of content as a non-hierarchical overlay that helps to gather, develop and navigate ideas. *Short.* A curated entry note that gathers annotated links to related documents in a subject area and serves agents as navigation. *Begriff.* Map of Content (MOC). *Kurz.* Kuratierter Einstiegspunkt in einen Themenbereich, der Links auf verwandte Dokumente mit kurzer Erläuterung versammelt und Agents zur Navigation dient.

- **Knowledge Document.** German Wissensdokument (Skriptum DE §5.4). Its properties are a clearly delimited subject, a traceable structure and dual readability for people and models. *Source.* Skriptum DE §5.4 with the definition and the property list; Notes EN, knowledge-document section; CLARIAH, "Markdown and Knowledge Documents". *Literature.* Pollin 2025, "Promptotyping. The CLARIAH wording, an atomic, structured and verifiable unit of externalised project knowledge, is recorded as instance wording of that profile; the VetMedAI script carries the same atomic reading in German. Atomic and verifiable make claims that wording deliberately does not make, since a knowledge document is bounded by purpose rather than by size and is revised rather than proven. *Short.* A bounded, structured and revisable representation of relevant knowledge, distilled from a larger body, checked by people and usable as context by LLM-based systems. *Begriff.* Wissensdokument. *Kurz.* Eine begrenzte, strukturierte und revidierbare Repräsentation relevanten Wissens, aus einem größeren Bestand destilliert, von Menschen geprüft und als Kontext für LLM-basierte Systeme nutzbar.
- **Instruction File.** German Instruktionsdatei (Skriptum DE §5.6). It is procedural where a knowledge document is descriptive. *Source.* Skriptum DE §5.6 with edition-specific examples; Notes EN, "Agent Skill"; Deck. *Literature.* Agent Skills specification (agentskills.io); Anthropic, Agent Skills overview. *Editorial decision 2026-08-20.* Progressive disclosure stays with the skill, which is the artefact carrying the mechanism, and is no longer cited as an anchor of the knowledge-document definition. *Short.* A reusable folder with SKILL.md and resources for one recurring task class, loaded in stages so only name and description occupy context until relevant. *Begriff.* Agent Skill. *Kurz.* Ein wiederverwendbarer Ordner mit einer SKILL.md und Ressourcen für eine wiederkehrende Aufgabenklasse, der stufenweise geladen wird, sodass bis zu seiner Relevanz nur Name und Beschreibung Kontext belegen.
- **Markdown as Serialization.** German Markdown als technische Repräsentation (Skriptum DE §5.5); the Deck phrases it as Serialisierung. The claim that the knowledge document is the conceptual artefact while Markdown is one possible technical representation of it, chosen for openness, versionability, linkability and direct readability by people and models. The material makes no claim of superiority over richer formats and keeps TEI, JSON, RDF, XML, schemas and databases for cases requiring richer semantics or formal constraints. *Source.* Skriptum DE §5.5; CLARIAH, "Markdown and Knowledge Documents"; Notes EN, "Markdown Makes Document Structure Explicit for LLMs"; Deck. *Literature.* Markdown Guide. *Short.* The knowledge document as conceptual artefact and Markdown as one possible representation, chosen for openness, versionability, linkability and readability by people and LLMs. *Begriff.* Markdown als technische Repräsentation. *Kurz.* Das Wissensdokument als konzeptuelles Artefakt und Markdown als eine mögliche technische Repräsentation, gewählt wegen Offenheit, Versionierbarkeit, Verlinkbarkeit und Lesbarkeit für Menschen und LLMs.
- **Promptotyping Documents (Declarative, Process and Action Documents).** German Promptotyping Documents, with the types Declarative Documents, Process Documents and Action Documents, all untranslated (UFL, "Promptotyping Documents"). Knowledge documents sorted by the kind of knowledge they carry. Declarative Documents hold knowledge about the subject, its data, domain and requirements, for example `project.md`, `data.md`, `specification.md` and `design.md`, and extend what the LLM knows about the project. Process Documents hold knowledge about the course of the work, what has happened, what is open and what comes next, and together with Git `journal.md`, `handoff.md` and `plan.md` form the memory of the project. Action Documents hold knowledge about acting, what agents may do in the project and how, through instruction files, `governance.md`, `rules.md` and skills, and so steer the agent's behaviour. The typology is analytical and serves diagnosis, since factually wrong output points first to the Declarative Documents and formally wrong output to the Action Documents. The Promptotyping specification now carries the class under the generic name knowledge document and records Promptotyping Document as its former name. *Source.* UFL, "Promptotyping Documents", which cites the Promptotyping monograph (Pollin 2026); this repository's `knowledge/` folder follows the typology. *Literature.* DHCraft, Promptotyping specification, glossary entries Knowledge Document, Declarative Document, Process Document and Action Document. *Short.* Knowledge documents classified by the knowledge they carry, about the subject (declarative), the course of work (process) or acting (action). *Begriff.* Promptotyping Documents. *Kurz.* Wissensdokumente, unterschieden nach der Art des Wissens, über den Gegenstand (Declarative), den Arbeitsverlauf (Process) oder das Handeln (Action).
- **Frontmatter.** German Frontmatter, in the UFL notes also Kopf (UFL, "Das Wissensdokument im Vault"). The block of key-value pairs in YAML between two lines of three hyphens at the top of a Markdown file, which Obsidian presents as the note's properties. It holds structured data such as type, dates, status and tags and makes a collection of documents queryable for people and agents. In the UFL notes it is the head of a knowledge document in the vault, beside a body with one self-contained concept per file and relations expressed as wikilinks. *Source.* UFL, "Das Wissensdokument im Vault" and "Tags und Tag-Taxonomie"; Hands-on 3, where the agent proposes and applies a frontmatter schema; "Context Engineering", where frontmatter appears under processing context. *Literature.* Obsidian Help, "Properties". *Short.* A YAML block of key-value pairs at the top of a Markdown file that records metadata such as type, date, status and tags. *Begriff.* Frontmatter. *Kurz.* YAML-Block aus Schlüssel und Wert am Anfang einer Markdown-Datei, der Metadaten wie Typ, Datum, Status und Tags festhält.
- **Wikilink.** German Wikilink (UFL, "Das Wissensdokument im Vault"). An internal link written as a note name in double square brackets, the default link format of Obsidian, which can point to a heading or a block and can carry a display text after a vertical bar. Obsidian can update such links when a file is renamed and shows which documents link to a given one. In the UFL notes wikilinks carry the relations of a knowledge document, in the running text and under a Related section, and they are the paths an agent follows from a map of content through a vault too large for its context window. Dead wikilinks are a target of structural curation. *Source.* UFL, "Das Wissensdokument im Vault", "Maps of Content", Hands-on 3 and "Kuratieren: Struktur und Inhalt". *Literature.* Obsidian Help, "Internal links". *Short.* An internal link written as a note name in double square brackets, connecting documents in a vault. *Begriff.* Wikilink. *Kurz.* Interner Link in doppelten eckigen Klammern, der Dokumente in einem Vault miteinander verbindet.

## 4 Agentic Engineering

### Agents, harness and execution

- **Agentic Engineering.** German Agentic Engineering, untranslated (Skriptum DE, chapter 6). The systematic organisation and control of multi-step agentic work, covering the delimitation and decomposition of tasks, tool use, the handling of intermediate results, states and handovers, stopping and escalation conditions, and checking and continuation. The material scope is wider than agentic software development, since the work addresses data descriptions, specifications, mappings, design decisions, process documents and verification concepts alongside code. *Source.* Skriptum DE §6.1; Notes EN, "Agentic Engineering"; Deck, which states the widening explicitly. *Literature.* Karpathy 2026, Sequoia Ascent account of the coinage; AGENT 2026 workshop at ICSE; Hassan et al. 2025 (arXiv:2509.06216); Sapkota, Roumeliotis and Karkee 2025 (DOI 10.1016/j.inffus.2025.103599); Pollin, *Promptotyping* (monograph, review draft 0.9, 2026) for the operative definition. *Short.* The systematic organisation and control of multi-step agentic work, from task delimitation and tool use through intermediate states and handovers to stopping, escalation and checking. *Begriff.* Agentic Engineering. *Kurz.* Die systematische Organisation und Steuerung mehrschrittiger agentischer Arbeit, von der Abgrenzung von Aufgaben und der Werkzeugnutzung über Zwischenstände und Übergaben bis zu Abbruch, Eskalation und Prüfung.
- **AI Agent and Agentic AI.** German AI Agent, untranslated (Skriptum DE §2.3); Agentic AI untranslated. An agent pursues a goal across several model and tool calls, examining its environment, selecting an action, observing the result and updating its approach, with the model as the flexible planning and interpretation module whose actual scope of action arises from tools and a working environment. The received distinction treats agentic systems as the umbrella, workflows as orchestration through predefined code paths, and agents as systems that direct their own processes and tool use; agentic AI names the wider paradigm of multi-agent collaboration, dynamic task decomposition, persistent memory and coordinated autonomy. The received advice starts from the simplest solution and moves to workflows for well-defined tasks that need predictability and to agents for open-ended problems whose steps cannot be fixed in advance. The UFL notes carry the distinction into delegation, where the dynamic workflow is an orchestration that the model itself writes (see Subagent). *Source.* Skriptum DE §2.3 with the nine-step edition example; Notes EN and Deck; VetMedAI, chatbot against agent contrast.; UFL, "LLM-basierte AI Agents" and "Subagents und Workflows". *Literature.* Anthropic 2024, "Building effective agents"; Wooldridge and Jennings 1995 (DOI 10.1017/S0269888900008122); Sapkota, Roumeliotis and Karkee 2025 (DOI 10.1016/j.inffus.2025.103599). *Short.* An agent pursues a goal across several model and tool calls, observing results and updating its approach, while agentic AI names the wider multi-agent paradigm. *Begriff.* AI Agent und Agentic AI. *Kurz.* Ein Agent verfolgt ein Ziel über mehrere Modell- und Werkzeugaufrufe hinweg, beobachtet Ergebnisse und passt sein Vorgehen an, während Agentic AI das weitere Paradigma der Zusammenarbeit mehrerer Agenten bezeichnet.
- **AI Harness.** German AI Harness, untranslated (Skriptum DE §2.4). The technical software layer through which an LLM-based agent receives context, calls tools, accesses files, executes programs and processes feedback, and which sets what may be read or changed, which commands run without confirmation, how tool output returns into the model context, how long a run continues, when a person is involved and how intermediate results are stored. It provides technical control and decides nothing about scholarly adequacy. *Source.* Skriptum DE §2.4 with the control list and the validator example; CLARIAH, "Agentic Engineering and AI Harnesses"; Notes EN, "AI Harness Architecture". *Literature.* Zhong and Zhu 2026, "AI Harness Engineering" (arXiv:2605.13357); Anthropic 2025, "Effective harnesses for long-running agents"; Martin 2026, "Agent Harness Design"; METR 2024, capability elicitation. *Editorial decision 2026-08-20.* The anchor sets are merged. Zhong and Zhu is the paper all four mined source texts use for the harness and was missing from the earlier matrix; the practitioner and evaluation anchors are kept for the component-level and measurement-level readings. *Short.* The software layer through which an LLM-based agent receives context, calls tools, accesses files and processes feedback, providing technical control without deciding scholarly adequacy. *Begriff.* AI Harness. *Kurz.* Die Softwareschicht, über die ein LLM-basierter Agent Kontext erhält, Werkzeuge aufruft, auf Dateien zugreift und Feedback verarbeitet, und die technische Kontrolle bietet, ohne über wissenschaftliche Angemessenheit zu entscheiden.
- **Compound System (Model, Harness and Environment).** German Verbundsystem (proposal); the Skriptum states the matter in §2.3 and §2.4 without a term. The claim that agentic capability is a property of model, harness and environment together, so that the unit of analysis is the complete system of model, harness, context, tools, state, permissions, executable environment and feedback. *Source.* CLARIAH, "Agentic Engineering and AI Harnesses" with the explicit formula; Notes EN, "AI Harness Architecture"; Deck. *Literature.* Zhong and Zhu 2026 (arXiv:2605.13357). *Short.* Agentic capability as a joint property of model, harness and environment, making the whole system of context, tools, state, permissions and feedback the analytical unit. *Begriff.* Verbundsystem. *Kurz.* Agentische Fähigkeit als gemeinsame Eigenschaft von Modell, Harness und Umgebung, sodass das vollständige System aus Kontext, Werkzeugen, Zustand, Berechtigungen und Feedback die Analyseeinheit bildet.
- **Tool Use.** German Werkzeugnutzung (Skriptum DE §6.1 and §6.4); Tool Use on the Deck. - **Trajectory.** German Arbeitstrajektorie (Skriptum DE §3.7 and §6.1; Deck). The complete sequence of observations, intermediate decisions, tool calls, file modifications, execution results and follow-up actions that an agentic run produces, which the material makes the object of engineering in place of the single response. *Source.* Notes EN, "Agentic Engineering" with the shift from response to trajectory; Skriptum DE §6.1 with the propagating-error example; Deck. *Literature.* Zhong and Zhu 2026 (arXiv:2605.13357), for the argument that a final artefact is weak evidence about the process that produced it. *Short.* The complete sequence of observations, decisions, tool calls, file modifications, execution results and follow-up actions of an agentic run, which becomes the object of engineering. *Begriff.* Arbeitstrajektorie. *Kurz.* Die vollständige Folge von Beobachtungen, Entscheidungen, Werkzeugaufrufen, Dateiänderungen, Ausführungsergebnissen und Folgeaktionen eines agentischen Laufs, die zum Gegenstand des Engineering wird.
- **Agentic Execution Loop.** German Agentische Ausführungsschleife (Skriptum DE §6.2 with figure 10). The repeated cycle of registering the current state, planning a bounded next step, executing a tool or action, observing the result and updating the approach, bound to documented requirements, permissions, stopping conditions and human intervention points. *Source.* Skriptum DE §6.2 with the ten-step edition example; Notes EN, "Agentic Engineering"; Deck, hands-on step 1. *Literature.* No single canonical source; the Deck relates the loop to the cybernetic feedback loop (Wiener 1948) and notes the difference that feedback arrives through tool output rather than sensors. *Short.* The repeated cycle of registering state, planning a bounded step, acting, observing and updating the approach, bound to requirements, permissions, stopping conditions and intervention points. *Begriff.* Agentische Ausführungsschleife. *Kurz.* Der wiederholte Zyklus aus Erfassen des Zustands, Planen eines begrenzten Schritts, Handeln, Beobachten und Anpassen des Vorgehens, gebunden an Anforderungen, Berechtigungen, Abbruchbedingungen und Interventionspunkte.
- **Autonomy.** German Autonomie (Skriptum DE §6.2; Deck). The extent of work performed between two human interventions. Autonomy in this sense is compatible with full human control. *Source.* Skriptum DE §6.2, closing sentence; Deck, on the classical four properties. *Literature.* Wooldridge and Jennings 1995 (DOI 10.1017/S0269888900008122). *Editorial decision 2026-08-20.* The Skriptum reading is canonical, a scope of work between two human interventions. This is a deliberate narrowing against the classical agent-systems property, where autonomy is a system property alongside reactivity, pro-activeness and social ability. Both readings are defensible and they are not congruent; a text using the classical sense states it. *Short.* The extent of work performed between two human interventions, a reading compatible with full human control and narrower than the classical agent-systems property. *Begriff.* Autonomie. *Kurz.* Der Umfang der Arbeit, die zwischen zwei menschlichen Interventionen geleistet wird, eine Lesart, die mit voller menschlicher Kontrolle vereinbar und enger als die klassische Eigenschaft von Agentensystemen ist.
- **Harness Engineering.** German Harness Engineering, untranslated (UFL, "Prompt Engineering ist so 2025!"). The design and checking of an agent's working environment with its tools, knowledge, permissions and control. The UFL notes hold that with current models the form of a prompt matters little, since goal, required knowledge and success criteria decide, and that quality arises increasingly outside the prompt, in the context and in the environment that steers and checks the work. Harness engineering is the third of the five layers through which the notes order agentic engineering, prompt, knowledge and context, harness, loop and factory, and permission modes and hooks are its instruments in the workshop. *Source.* UFL, "Prompt Engineering ist so 2025!", "Agentic Engineering im Überblick", "Permission Modes und Autonomie" and "Hook: nur im Projektordner arbeiten". *Literature.* No source read for this entry; the five-layer sequence follows a video by IBM Technology (2026) that the UFL notes cite, and the AI Harness entry carries the harness literature. *Short.* The design and checking of the environment in which an AI agent works, including its tools, knowledge, permissions and controls. *Begriff.* Harness Engineering. *Kurz.* Gestaltung und Prüfung der Arbeitsumgebung eines Agenten mit Werkzeugen, Wissen, Berechtigungen und Steuerung.
- **Loop Engineering.** German Loop Engineering, untranslated (UFL, "Loop Engineering"). The design of the system that triggers an agent and checks its work, so that the agent no longer has to be prompted by hand each time. A trigger such as a new file, a point in time or an event starts the agent; a fixed check script then runs, which the agent cannot skip; a deviation starts an inner loop with a new attempt; and only the checked result goes to a person for release. Since every trigger multiplies requests and tokens, stopping conditions and limits belong to the design. The agentic execution loop describes the agent's own cycle, while loop engineering shapes the triggers and fixed checks around it. It is the fourth of the five layers prompt, knowledge and context, harness, loop and factory. *Source.* UFL, "Loop Engineering" with the Hands-on 4 diagram, and "Agentic Engineering im Überblick"; Deck appendix, which relates the term tentatively to the cybernetic feedback loop. *Literature.* No source read for this entry; the UFL notes take the term from a video by IBM Technology (2026). *Short.* The design of the system that triggers an AI agent and checks its results with fixed tests before a person releases them. *Begriff.* Loop Engineering. *Kurz.* Gestaltung des Systems, das den Agenten anstößt und seine Ergebnisse mit festen Prüfungen kontrolliert, bevor ein Mensch sie freigibt.

### Control and delegation

- **Least-Privilege Tool Access.** German Prinzip der geringsten erforderlichen Berechtigung (Skriptum DE §6.4). The rule that tool and file access is granted at the minimum required scope, with source files readable but not writable, generated artefacts confined to a working folder, validators running without confirmation, and publication or deployment steps requiring explicit release. *Source.* Skriptum DE §6.4 with the edition-specific permission table; CLARIAH and Notes EN, "Prepare the Working Environment before the First Prompt". *Literature.* Saltzer and Schroeder 1975 (DOI 10.1109/PROC.1975.9939), proposed anchor for the principle, not cited in the scripts. *Short.* The rule that tool and file access is granted at the minimum required scope, with sources read-only and publication or deployment requiring explicit release. *Begriff.* Prinzip der geringsten erforderlichen Berechtigung. *Kurz.* Die Regel, dass Werkzeug- und Dateizugriff im kleinsten erforderlichen Umfang gewährt wird, mit nur lesbaren Quellen und ausdrücklicher Freigabe für Veröffentlichung oder Deployment.
- **Intervention Point and Escalation Condition.** German Menschliche Interventionspunkte, Eskalationsbedingungen (Skriptum DE §6.1 and §6.6). The defined places at which an agentic run hands back to a person, named as contradictory requirements, missing disciplinary grounds, changes that are hard to reverse, sensitive resources, modelling decisions with disciplinary consequences, and validation and acceptance. *Source.* Skriptum DE §6.6 with the reversibility requirement for intermediate states; Notes EN, "Why Multi Step Work Must Be Organised". *Literature.* Anthropic 2024, "Building effective agents", on human checkpoints and guardrails. *Short.* The defined places where an agentic run hands back to a person, such as contradictory requirements, hard-to-reverse changes, sensitive resources, disciplinary modelling decisions and acceptance. *Begriff.* Menschliche Interventionspunkte und Eskalationsbedingungen. *Kurz.* Die festgelegten Stellen, an denen ein agentischer Lauf an eine Person zurückgibt, etwa bei widersprüchlichen Anforderungen, schwer umkehrbaren Änderungen, sensiblen Ressourcen, fachlich folgenreichen Modellierungsentscheidungen und Acceptance.
- **Subagent.** German Subagent (Skriptum DE §6.5; Deck). A delegated agent instance with a bounded partial task, its own fresh context and a defined return format, used to keep large volumes of intermediate information out of the parent context and to run parallelisable checks. It is an architectural pattern without a formal standard, and more agents raise coordination and verification effort. Independent review distributes inspection and exposes disagreement, while model agreement produces no truth by voting. It carries its own instructions and tool set. In Claude Code a custom subagent is a Markdown file with a short frontmatter head that names it, describes when it applies and limits its tools and model, and Claude Code ships Explore, Plan and General-purpose, while Codex ships the roles `explorer` and `worker`, with custom agents in `.codex/agents/`. A subagent with read-only tools serves as an independent reviewer (see Model-Assisted Review). In Anthropic's research system agents used about four times and multi-agent systems about fifteen times the tokens of a chat, and multi-agent work fits poorly where all agents need the same context or the task is hard to parallelise. Beside the subagent the UFL notes name two further forms of delegation, the dynamic workflow, in which the model writes an orchestration script that distributes subtasks to parallel subagents and has other agents cross-check the findings, and parallel sessions with their own folders that message one another. *Source.* Skriptum DE §6.5 with four edition-specific subagent roles; Notes EN, "Subagents" and "Subagents and Epistemic Infrastructure"; Deck.; UFL, "Subagents: ein Reviewer Agent" with the reviewer file and "Subagents und Workflows". *Literature.* Anthropic, "Create custom subagents"; Anthropic 2025, "How we built our multi-agent research system"; Anthropic 2026, "Introducing dynamic workflows in Claude Code"; OpenAI, "Subagents" (Codex documentation). The Deck states that the subagent is the one item among the five agent concepts without a formal specification. *Short.* A delegated agent instance with a bounded partial task, fresh context and defined return format, keeping intermediate information out of the parent context. *Begriff.* Subagent. *Kurz.* Eine delegierte Agenteninstanz mit begrenzter Teilaufgabe, eigenem frischem Kontext und definiertem Rückgabeformat, die Zwischeninformation aus dem übergeordneten Kontext heraushält.
- **Model Routing.** German Model Routing, untranslated (Deck). The assignment of different phases of an agentic workflow to different models and inference budgets, typically a stronger reasoning model for planning and review and a faster model for implementation, treated as an engineering decision that changes as models change. *Source.* Notes EN, "Model Routing" with the sequence from research through specification, implementation and review to revision; Deck. *Literature.* Practitioner provenance, no canonical source. *Short.* The assignment of different phases of an agentic workflow to different models and inference budgets, an engineering decision that changes as models change. *Begriff.* Model Routing. *Kurz.* Die Zuordnung verschiedener Phasen eines agentischen Workflows zu verschiedenen Modellen und Inferenzbudgets, eine Engineering-Entscheidung, die sich mit den Modellen ändert.
- **Model Context Protocol (MCP).** German Model Context Protocol (MCP), untranslated (Skriptum DE §6.5; Deck). An open standard connecting AI applications with external tools, data sources and workflows through common client and server interactions. It addresses an integration problem and decides nothing about relevance, reliability or permissibility of a resource. *Source.* Skriptum DE §6.5 with footnote 5 on implementation-dependent permission architecture; Notes EN, "Model Context Protocol (MCP)"; Deck, with the M-times-N argument. *Literature.* Model Context Protocol specification (modelcontextprotocol.io). *Short.* An open standard connecting AI applications with external tools, data sources and workflows, which addresses integration and decides nothing about a resource's relevance or reliability. *Begriff.* Model Context Protocol (MCP). *Kurz.* Ein offener Standard, der KI-Anwendungen mit externen Werkzeugen, Datenquellen und Workflows verbindet, ein Integrationsproblem adressiert und nichts über Relevanz oder Verlässlichkeit einer Ressource entscheidet.
- **Agent2Agent Protocol (A2A).** German Agent2Agent (A2A), untranslated (Deck); the Skriptum speaks of Agent-to-Agent-Protokollen (§6.5). An open protocol for discovery, task management and result exchange among independent agents without exposing internal memory, tools or proprietary logic, complementary to MCP in standardising agent-to-agent rather than agent-to-tool interaction. *Source.* Notes EN, "A2A (Agent to Agent)"; Skriptum DE §6.5 with the four methodological questions on responsibility, handover, conflict and decision authority; Deck. *Literature.* Agent2Agent Protocol specification (a2a-protocol.org). *Short.* An open protocol for discovery, task management and result exchange among independent agents without exposing their internal memory, tools or logic, complementing MCP's agent-to-tool standard. *Begriff.* Agent2Agent (A2A). *Kurz.* Ein offenes Protokoll für Auffindung, Aufgabenverwaltung und Ergebnisaustausch zwischen unabhängigen Agenten, ohne deren internen Speicher, Werkzeuge oder Logik offenzulegen, als Ergänzung zu MCP für die Interaktion zwischen Agent und Werkzeug.
- **Epistemic Infrastructure.** German Epistemische Infrastruktur (Deck). The arrangement in which knowledge documents, source data, schemas, tests, software tools, agents, project state and human judgment together establish the conditions under which a generated representation can be inspected, criticised, validated and accepted for a stated purpose. The concept carries the conclusion of both English texts. *Source.* CLARIAH, "AI-Supported Research Data Workflows & Epistemic Infrastructures" and "Agentic Engineering and AI Harnesses"; Notes EN, "Critical Expert and Epistemic Infrastructure"; Deck. *Literature.* Pollin, *Promptotyping* (monograph, review draft 0.9, 2026); Edwards et al. 2007, proposed wider anchor for the infrastructure-studies reading, which the scripts do not invoke. *Short.* Knowledge documents, data, schemas, tests, tools, agents and human judgment arranged so that a generated representation becomes inspectable, validatable and acceptable for a stated purpose. *Begriff.* Epistemische Infrastruktur. *Kurz.* Das Gefüge aus Wissensdokumenten, Daten, Schemata, Tests, Werkzeugen, Agenten und menschlichem Urteil, das eine generierte Repräsentation für einen benannten Zweck prüfbar, validierbar und akzeptierbar macht.
- **Agents Rule of Two.** German Rule of Two, untranslated (UFL, "KI-Governance auf zwei Ebenen"). A design rule for agent security under which an agent holds at most two of three properties within one session, processing untrustworthy inputs, accessing sensitive systems or private data, and changing state or communicating externally. A task that needs all three requires human approval or another reliable form of validation before the agent proceeds. The rule answers prompt injection, which its authors treat as unsolved, and it covers neither excessive privileges nor hallucination, so least privilege and defence in depth remain necessary. The UFL notes make it the core rule of governance on the agent level. *Source.* UFL, "KI-Governance auf zwei Ebenen"; the concept comes from Meta AI 2025, which names the lethal trifecta as its inspiration. *Literature.* Meta AI 2025, "Agents Rule of Two". *Short.* A security rule allowing an AI agent at most two of untrusted input, private data access and external action per session. *Begriff.* Rule of Two. *Kurz.* Sicherheitsregel, nach der ein AI Agent pro Session höchstens zwei von drei Eigenschaften verbindet, nämlich nicht vertrauenswürdige Eingaben, Zugriff auf private Daten und Handeln nach außen.
- **Permission Mode.** German Permission Mode, untranslated (UFL, "Permission Modes und Autonomie"). A harness setting that fixes which actions an agent performs without asking, and so how much autonomy it receives in a session; Claude Code runs with the rights of the person who starts it. Claude Code documents a graded set, from a planning mode that explores before anything changes, through a manual mode that asks for everything beyond reading and a mode that accepts file edits, to an auto mode in which a separate classifier model reviews actions beyond reading and editing in the project, and a bypass mode reserved for isolated environments. Writes to protected paths such as `.git` and `.claude` are never approved automatically outside the bypass mode, and deny rules hold in every mode. The documentation states that the auto mode reduces prompts without guaranteeing safety, so an enforced boundary needs a deny rule or a hook. *Source.* UFL, "Permission Modes und Autonomie", which teaches five modes; the Claude Code documentation additionally lists a `dontAsk` mode for locked-down scripted runs. *Literature.* Anthropic, "Choose a permission mode". *Short.* A harness setting that determines which actions an AI agent may take without asking, and thus how much autonomy it has. *Begriff.* Permission Mode. *Kurz.* Einstellung im AI Harness, die festlegt, was der Agent ohne Rückfrage tun darf, und damit seine Autonomie bestimmt.
- **Hook.** German Hook (UFL, "Hook: nur im Projektordner arbeiten"). A user-defined handler, typically a shell script, that the harness runs automatically at a defined point of the agent's lifecycle, for example before every file-writing tool call. A handler that ends with exit code 2 blocks the call, and its message returns to the model as the reason. The UFL notes set it against an instruction file, whose rules the model reads and can miss, while the harness executes a hook whatever the model decides; the documentation calls this deterministic control. A hook reaches only what its matcher covers, so a hook on the file tools leaves shell commands unchecked, and by default a failing hook lets the action proceed. The documentation recommends the permission system for a hard allow or deny, and full isolation requires a sandbox. *Source.* UFL, "Hook: nur im Projektordner arbeiten", with the script `workshops/2026-11-09-ufl/data/hooks/schutz.py`; Notes EN, "Global CLAUDE.md", on the separation of behavioural guidance from permissions and hooks. *Literature.* Anthropic, "Automate actions with hooks". *Short.* A script the AI harness runs automatically at a defined event, which can block an agent's action independently of the model. *Begriff.* Hook. *Kurz.* Skript, das der AI Harness bei einem festgelegten Ereignis ausführt und das eine Aktion des Agenten unabhängig vom Modell blockieren kann.
- **Deterministic and Probabilistic Tasks (Script versus LLM Division).** German deterministisch und probabilistisch (UFL, "Determinismus so weit wie möglich ausreizen"). The distinction between tasks with exactly one correct answer, which code, formulas and scripts solve with the same output for the same input, such as sums, budgets, dates, counts, completeness checks and format conversion, and tasks that call for judgment, where an LLM or a person can answer the same input differently, such as summarising, wording, classifying, weighing and deciding. The rule drawn from it is that whatever a script can do a script does, and the LLM writes the script and leaves the calculation to it. The Promptotyping specification draws the same line along unambiguity. *Source.* UFL, "Determinismus so weit wie möglich ausreizen" and the second prompt of Hands-on 4; "LLMs bzw. AI Agents als Jagged Alien Intelligences" for the probabilistic character of LLMs. *Literature.* DHCraft, Promptotyping specification, glossary entry Script versus LLM Division. *Short.* The division of tasks into those a script solves with one correct answer and those requiring the judgment of an LLM or person. *Begriff.* Deterministische und probabilistische Aufgaben. *Kurz.* Unterscheidung zwischen Aufgaben mit genau einer richtigen Antwort, die ein Skript löst, und Aufgaben, die ein Urteil von LLM oder Mensch verlangen.

### Promptotyping

- **Promptotyping.** German Promptotyping, untranslated (Skriptum DE, chapter 7). An iterative, document-based method for developing project-specific digital research artefacts with LLM-based agents, in which documented project understanding, artefact, checking procedures and bounded grounds of acceptance develop together. The cycle runs from project knowledge through working context and agentic implementation to a digital research artefact, its checking and the revision of the project knowledge. *Source.* Skriptum DE §7.1 with figure 11; CLARIAH, "Promptotyping"; Notes EN, "Promptotyping as an Iterative Knowledge Loop". *Literature.* Pollin, *Promptotyping* (monograph, review draft 0.9, 2026); Pollin 2025, "Promptotyping. Von der Idee zur Anwendung"; Pollin, Steyer, Scholger and Schiller-Stoff 2025, DHd (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.14943178); Pollin 2026, L.I.S.A.; DHCraft, Promptotyping specification. *Editorial decision 2026-08-20.* The monograph in review is added as an anchor. It is the load-bearing source for working context, project knowledge base, distillation, acceptance and epistemic infrastructure, and the earlier matrix did not list it. Until publication it is cited as a review draft. *Short.* An iterative, document-based method for developing project-specific digital research artefacts with LLM-based agents, in which project understanding, artefact, checks and grounds of acceptance develop together. *Begriff.* Promptotyping. *Kurz.* Eine iterative, dokumentbasierte Methode zur Entwicklung projektspezifischer digitaler Forschungsartefakte mit LLM-basierten Agenten, in der dokumentiertes Projektverständnis, Artefakt, Prüfverfahren und begrenzte Gründe der Acceptance gemeinsam entstehen.
- **Promptotyping Phase Model.** German Preparation, Exploration, Distillation, Implementation as chapter headings (Skriptum DE §7.2 to §7.6). The recurring work forms of the method, preparing sources and workspace into a reconstructible initial state, exploring what data and sources permit and constrain, distilling the developed understanding into maintained documents, and implementing the artefact so that gaps in the knowledge become visible. *Source.* Skriptum DE, chapter 7; CLARIAH, "Promptotyping" with the five-stage flow diagram; Notes EN, "Promptotyping as an Iterative Knowledge Loop". *Literature.* Pollin, *Promptotyping* (monograph, review draft 0.9, 2026); DHCraft, Promptotyping specification, which is the source of truth for the current phase model. The Deck's hands-on runs a different five-step sequence of Preparation, Planning, Feedback and Self Revision, Implementation, Verification; that sequence is a workshop exercise and does not restate the phase model. *Short.* The method's recurring work forms, preparing sources and workspace, exploring what data permit, distilling understanding into documents and implementing the artefact to expose knowledge gaps. *Begriff.* Phasenmodell des Promptotyping. *Kurz.* Die wiederkehrenden Arbeitsformen der Methode, die Vorbereitung von Quellen und Arbeitsumgebung, die Exploration dessen, was die Daten erlauben, die Distillation des Verständnisses in Dokumente und die Implementierung des Artefakts, die Wissenslücken sichtbar macht.
- **Promptotype.** German Promptotype (Skriptum DE §7.10). The documented and referenceable state of one iteration, comprising the relevant state of the knowledge base, the artefact, the referenced data state, the documented checks, the open questions and the bounded grounds of acceptance. It is a sufficiently determined state to continue from, and acceptance attaches to it. *Source.* Skriptum DE §7.10. *Literature.* Pollin, *Promptotyping* (monograph, review draft 0.9, 2026). *Short.* The documented, referenceable state of one iteration, comprising knowledge base, artefact, data state, checks, open questions and bounded grounds of acceptance, to which acceptance attaches. *Begriff.* Promptotype. *Kurz.* Der dokumentierte, referenzierbare Stand einer Iteration aus Wissensbasis, Artefakt, Datenstand, Prüfungen, offenen Fragen und begrenzten Gründen der Acceptance, an den die Acceptance gebunden ist.
- **Scholar-Centred Design.** German Scholar-Centred Design, untranslated (Skriptum DE §7.5; Deck). The orientation of development towards the research practices, interpretative tasks and responsibilities of the participating scholars, asking which scholarly distinctions an interface keeps visible beyond whether it is usable. *Source.* Skriptum DE §7.5; Notes EN, "Scholar Centred Design and Requirements Engineering"; Deck. *Literature.* Pollin 2025, *Modelling, Operationalising and Exploring Historical Information* (dissertation, Graz). *Short.* The orientation of development towards the research practices, interpretative tasks and responsibilities of scholars, asking which scholarly distinctions an interface keeps visible. *Begriff.* Scholar-Centred Design. *Kurz.* Die Ausrichtung der Entwicklung an den Forschungspraktiken, Interpretationsaufgaben und Verantwortlichkeiten der beteiligten Wissenschaftler:innen, mit der Frage, welche fachlichen Unterscheidungen eine Oberfläche sichtbar hält.
- **User Story.** German User Story, untranslated (Deck). A requirements format stating who acts, which capability is required and why it matters, used in the material as the bridge between structured research data and an implementable artefact, because it is readable by people and usable as agent context. *Source.* Notes EN, "As a … I Want to" with the liturgy and social-history examples; Deck, Scholar-Centred Design slide with worked stories. *Literature.* Pollin 2025, *Modelling, Operationalising and Exploring Historical Information* (dissertation, Graz). *Short.* A requirements format stating who acts, which capability is required and why it matters, bridging structured research data and an implementable artefact. *Begriff.* User Story. *Kurz.* Ein Anforderungsformat, das festhält, wer handelt, welche Fähigkeit benötigt wird und warum sie wichtig ist, als Brücke zwischen strukturierten Forschungsdaten und einem implementierbaren Artefakt.
- **Spec-Driven Development.** German spezifikationsgetriebene Entwicklung (proposal); the Deck uses the English term. The placement of an explicit specification between exploratory discussion and implementation, contextualised by the maintained knowledge base rather than written as a detached document, so that a reviewable object exists before code is produced. *Source.* Notes EN, "Spec Driven Development"; Deck, with the note that the specification is contextualised in the `knowledge` folder. *Literature.* Practitioner provenance, no canonical source. *Short.* The placement of an explicit specification, contextualised by the maintained knowledge base, between exploratory discussion and implementation, so that a reviewable object precedes code. *Begriff.* spezifikationsgetriebene Entwicklung. *Kurz.* Die Platzierung einer expliziten, durch die gepflegte Wissensbasis kontextualisierten Spezifikation zwischen explorativer Diskussion und Implementierung, sodass vor dem Code ein prüfbares Objekt vorliegt.
- **Write-back.** German Write-back, untranslated (Skriptum DE §7.9). The defined operation of returning findings from implementation and checking into the responsible documents, so that new knowledge stays out of the chat, the code and one person's memory. Findings are examined before they are written back. *Source.* Skriptum DE §7.9 and §7.11; CLARIAH, "Promptotyping" as curated feedback; Notes EN, "Promptotyping as an Iterative Knowledge Loop" as curated write back. *Literature.* Pollin, *Promptotyping* (monograph, review draft 0.9, 2026). *Short.* The defined operation of returning examined findings into the responsible documents, so new knowledge stays out of chat, code and individual memory. *Begriff.* Write-back. *Kurz.* Die festgelegte Operation, geprüfte Befunde in die zuständigen Dokumente zurückzuführen, damit neues Wissen nicht im Chat, im Code oder im Gedächtnis Einzelner verbleibt.
- **Fancy Research Tools.** German Fancy (Research) Tools, untranslated (UFL, "Fancy Research Tools"). Small research tools that coding agents build from a maintained knowledge base, mostly static applications that run in the browser without a server. The UFL notes describe the route in five steps, each with a named bearer. The person clarifies question and data; person and agent record project, data and requirements in `knowledge/`; the agent builds in milestones that the person releases; tests, a review agent and the person's disciplinary inspection check the result against the material; and the tool is used and maintained while the knowledge base grows. A finding from checking changes the knowledge base first and the code afterwards, and such a tool becomes professional through checking, maintenance and handover. *Source.* UFL, "Fancy Research Tools"; Hands-on 4 as a worked instance. *Literature.* Digital Humanities Craft, fancy (research) tools. *Short.* Small browser-based research tools that coding agents build from a maintained knowledge base, checked and released by people. *Begriff.* Fancy (Research) Tools. *Kurz.* Kleine Forschungswerkzeuge im Browser, die Coding Agents aus einer gepflegten Wissensbasis bauen und die Menschen prüfen und freigeben.

## 5 Critical Perspectives and Governance

### Checking and acceptance

The material cuts the checking vocabulary two ways, and the cut of the Full Lecture Notes is canonical. *Editorial decision 2026-08-20.* Technical Verification means conformity to specified or formalised requirements and Validation means adequacy to source, purpose and disciplinary context. The CLARIAH lecture notes swap the two words, which is documented as an instance variant of that profile rather than as a second vocabulary. A text in the CLARIAH wording maps onto the canonical wording as follows.

| Canonical (Full Lecture Notes) | CLARIAH wording |
| --- | --- |
| Evaluation | Evaluation, narrower, measurement against an explicit criterion |
| Technical Verification | Validation |
| Validation (scholarly, domain) | Scholarly verification |
| Acceptance | no corresponding term |

- **Verification Levels.** German Prüfstufen (proposal). The umbrella name of the earlier matrix for the ordered set of checks a generated artefact passes, retained here as a pointer to the four separate entries below. The material separates three kinds of object that a single ladder cannot hold together, a procedure (deterministic check, model-assisted review), a role with authority (Critical Expert) and a decision (Acceptance). The UFL notes teach the ladder as Qualität sichern through four mechanisms, independent review, a person in the process for what automatic tests miss, turning each found error into a permanent check, and rights and releases such as push, sending and publication that stay with the person. *Source.* Skriptum DE §7.7; Notes EN, "Evaluation, Verification, Validation and Acceptance"; CLARIAH, "Evaluation, Validation and Scholarly Verification".; UFL, "Qualität sichern". *Literature.* Wei 2025, asymmetry of verification, for the ordering principle; Zheng et al. 2023 (arXiv:2306.05685); Pollin 2025, "Promptotyping mit Claude Sonnet 4"; Anthropic 2024, "Building effective agents". *Short.* The earlier umbrella name for the ordered checks on an artefact, kept as pointer to separate entries for procedures, the Critical Expert role and acceptance. *Begriff.* Prüfstufen. *Kurz.* Der frühere Sammelname für die geordneten Prüfungen eines Artefakts, beibehalten als Verweis auf getrennte Einträge zu Verfahren, zur Rolle des Critical Expert und zur Acceptance.
- **Evaluation.** German Evaluation (Skriptum DE §3.5 for prompt evaluation). Measurement or assessment of outputs, models or workflows against explicit criteria, quantitative or qualitative, including benchmark scores, error rates, task-completion measures and structured expert review. *Source.* Notes EN, "Evaluation, Verification, Validation and Acceptance"; CLARIAH, "Evaluation, Validation and Scholarly Verification". *Literature.* Zheng et al. 2023 (arXiv:2306.05685), proposed anchor for the model-based case; the Full Lecture Notes mark the broad usage in their own footnote. *Short.* Measurement or assessment of outputs, models or workflows against explicit criteria, quantitative or qualitative, including benchmark scores, error rates and structured expert review. *Begriff.* Evaluation. *Kurz.* Messung oder Bewertung von Ausgaben, Modellen oder Workflows anhand expliziter Kriterien, quantitativ oder qualitativ, einschließlich Benchmark-Werten, Fehlerraten und strukturierter fachlicher Begutachtung.
- **Technical Verification.** German Technische Verifikation (Skriptum DE §7.7). The check whether an artefact conforms to specified or formalised requirements, through schema validation, deterministic mappings, test suites, numerical consistency checks and executable acceptance criteria, and therefore largely automatable because the conditions have been formalised. *Source.* Skriptum DE §7.7 with the edition-specific check list and the Verification Report template; Notes EN, "Evaluation, Verification, Validation and Acceptance". *Literature.* No external anchor; the Full Lecture Notes state that the distinction adapts established verification and validation terminology from software and systems engineering. *Short.* The check whether an artefact conforms to specified or formalised requirements, through schemas, tests and executable criteria, and therefore largely automatable. *Begriff.* Technische Verifikation. *Kurz.* Die Prüfung, ob ein Artefakt spezifizierten oder formalisierten Anforderungen entspricht, durch Schemata, Tests und ausführbare Kriterien, und daher weitgehend automatisierbar.
- **Validation (Scholarly or Domain Validation).** German Fachliche und wissenschaftliche Validierung (Skriptum DE §7.7). The check whether a representation, interpretation or artefact is adequate to the source, the purpose and the disciplinary context, which requires domain expertise and resists reduction to a deterministic test. A schema-valid document can encode a historically unjustified interpretation. *Source.* Skriptum DE §7.7 with its four guiding questions; Notes EN, same section; CLARIAH under the name scholarly verification. *Literature.* Pollin, *Promptotyping* (monograph, review draft 0.9, 2026). See the mapping note above for the CLARIAH wording. *Short.* The check whether a representation, interpretation or artefact is adequate to source, purpose and disciplinary context, which requires domain expertise and resists deterministic testing. *Begriff.* Fachliche und wissenschaftliche Validierung. *Kurz.* Die Prüfung, ob eine Repräsentation, Interpretation oder ein Artefakt Quelle, Zweck und fachlichem Kontext angemessen ist, die Fachexpertise erfordert und sich deterministischen Tests entzieht.
- **Acceptance.** German Acceptance, untranslated (Skriptum DE §7.7, §7.10, §7.11). The responsible decision to use an identified state for a named purpose, stated purpose-bound, since a technically verified artefact can be unsuitable for research use and an interesting demonstrator can be unready for publication. Acceptance is separate from publication. *Source.* Skriptum DE §7.7 and the Acceptance Statement template with its sections for identified state, accepted-for purpose, technically verified, disciplinarily checked, not demonstrated, open questions and responsible decision; Notes EN, same section. *Literature.* Pollin, *Promptotyping* (monograph, review draft 0.9, 2026). *Short.* The responsible decision to use an identified state for a named purpose, separate from technical verification and from publication. *Begriff.* Acceptance. *Kurz.* Die verantwortliche Entscheidung, einen identifizierten Stand für einen benannten Zweck zu nutzen, getrennt von technischer Verifikation und von Veröffentlichung.
- **Critical Expert.** German Critical Expert, untranslated (Skriptum DE §7.8). The responsible disciplinary authority in a project, defined by function rather than by person, which decides which reading is defensible, whether a model is adequate to the source, whether an interface preserves scholarly distinctions and whether a state is accepted for a named purpose. Agents and validators supply evidence for that decision, and the role designs the epistemic conditions rather than manually checking every output. *Source.* Skriptum DE §7.8; Notes EN, "Critical Expert and Epistemic Infrastructure". *Literature.* Pollin 2025, "Promptotyping mit Claude Sonnet 4. Vibe-Coding erfordert den Critical-Expert-in-the-Loop". *Short.* The responsible disciplinary authority in a project, defined by function, which decides which reading is defensible and whether a state is accepted for a purpose. *Begriff.* Critical Expert. *Kurz.* Die verantwortliche fachliche Instanz eines Projekts, über ihre Funktion bestimmt, die entscheidet, welche Lesart vertretbar ist und ob ein Stand für einen Zweck akzeptiert wird.
- **Model-Assisted Review.** German Agentisches Review (Skriptum DE, figure 12 caption); the UFL notes use the received name LLM-as-a-Judge. The inspection of one model's output by a second model with access to the source and to executable tools, which can surface possible errors and direct human attention while reproducing the same mistake, introducing a new one or overstating confidence. Model agreement is no scholarly verification. In the UFL form a subagent with fresh context judges the work against fixed criteria, and its findings are checked against the material before anything changes. *Source.* CLARIAH, "Model-Assisted Review inside an AI Harness"; Skriptum DE §7.7 with figure 12; Notes EN, "Subagents and Epistemic Infrastructure"; Deck, three-subagent TEI verification slide.; UFL, "Hands-on 1: Follow-Up Prompts zum Feedback" and "Qualität sichern". *Literature.* Zheng et al. 2023 (arXiv:2306.05685). *Short.* The inspection of an LLM's output by a second LLM with source and tool access, which directs attention, though its agreement is no scholarly validation. *Begriff.* Agentisches Review. *Kurz.* Die Prüfung der Ausgabe eines LLM durch ein zweites LLM mit Zugang zu Quelle und Werkzeugen, die Aufmerksamkeit lenkt, wobei die Übereinstimmung der Modelle keine fachliche Validierung darstellt.
- **Deterministic Check.** German Deterministische Prüfung (implied by Skriptum DE §7.7; the Deck uses deterministische Validation). A reproducible test over an explicit representation, such as recomputing row and column sums of a table or validating XML against a schema, which reveals internal inconsistency while establishing nothing about whether the source was interpreted correctly. The UFL notes generalise the check into a division of labour (see Deterministic and Probabilistic Tasks), and Hands-on 4 has every key figure recomputed by a reference script from the source files. *Source.* CLARIAH, "From Generated Text to Structured Data"; Notes EN, ".txt → JSON → CSV" with the pattern from probabilistic interpretation through structured representation to deterministic checking.; UFL, "Determinismus so weit wie möglich ausreizen" and Hands-on 4. *Literature.* Formulation of the teaching line. *Short.* A reproducible test over an explicit representation, such as recomputing table sums or schema validation, revealing internal inconsistency without establishing correct interpretation of the source. *Begriff.* Deterministische Prüfung. *Kurz.* Ein reproduzierbarer Test über einer expliziten Repräsentation, etwa die Nachrechnung von Tabellensummen oder eine Schemavalidierung, der innere Inkonsistenz aufdeckt, ohne die korrekte Interpretation der Quelle zu belegen.
- **Candidate Representation.** German Kandidatenrepräsentation (proposal), absent from both German sources. The status a model output holds before assessment, valuable as a plausible structured proposal while carrying no evidence of source fidelity. The material generalises the status beyond transcription to every representational transition in a workflow. *Source.* Notes EN, "Hands on 1 (Example Result)"; CLARIAH, "Example Result and Critical Review". *Literature.* Term of the teaching line. *Short.* The status of an LLM output before assessment, valuable as a plausible structured proposal while carrying no evidence of source fidelity. *Begriff.* Kandidatenrepräsentation. *Kurz.* Der Status einer LLM-Ausgabe vor ihrer Bewertung, wertvoll als plausibler strukturierter Vorschlag und zugleich ohne Beleg für Quellentreue.
- **AI Slop and De-Slopification.** German AI Slop, De-Slopification, untranslated (UFL, "Die erste Iteration des Dashboards"). Generic elements that LLMs produce unasked, in interfaces for example decorative key figures, gradient surfaces and promotional language, and the deliberate removal of such elements, which the UFL notes make part of every iteration, for interfaces and texts alike. *Source.* UFL, "Die erste Iteration des Dashboards" and the feedback prompt in "Hands-on 1: Follow-Up Prompts zum Feedback". *Literature.* No canonical source read for this entry. *Short.* Generic elements LLMs add unasked, such as decorative figures or promotional wording, and their deliberate removal in each iteration. *Begriff.* AI Slop und De-Slopification. *Kurz.* Generische Elemente, die LLMs ungefragt erzeugen, etwa dekorative Kennzahlen oder Werbesprache, und ihr gezieltes Entfernen in jeder Iteration.

### Openness and deployment

- **Openness as a Multidimensional Property.** German mehrdimensionale Offenheit (proposal), absent from the German sources, since the openness block exists only in the English lecture notes. The reading of openness as a set of separately gradable dimensions covering training data, documentation, training code, architecture, parameters, licences and access conditions, in place of a binary open or closed classification. *Source.* CLARIAH, "The Current LLM Capability Landscape". *Literature.* European Open Source AI Index (osai-index.eu), which evaluates fourteen dimensions. *Short.* Openness read as separately gradable dimensions covering training data, documentation, training code, architecture, parameters, licences and access conditions, replacing a binary open or closed classification. *Begriff.* mehrdimensionale Offenheit. *Kurz.* Offenheit verstanden als Satz getrennt abstufbarer Dimensionen zu Trainingsdaten, Dokumentation, Trainingscode, Architektur, Parametern, Lizenzen und Zugangsbedingungen, an Stelle einer binären Einteilung in offen oder geschlossen.
- **Open-Weight Model.** German Open-Weight-Modell (proposal). A model whose trained parameters are available under a specified licence and can be downloaded, adapted and executed independently. The material keeps the term strictly separate from open source. *Source.* CLARIAH, "The Current LLM Capability Landscape" and the cyber-capability passage on diffusion risk; Notes EN, "Which Frontier Models and AI Technologies Do You Use?". *Literature.* Open Source Initiative, The Open Source AI Definition 1.0. *Short.* A model whose trained parameters are available under a specified licence for download, adaptation and independent execution, kept strictly separate from open source. *Begriff.* Open-Weight-Modell. *Kurz.* Ein Modell, dessen trainierte Parameter unter einer festgelegten Lizenz zum Herunterladen, Anpassen und unabhängigen Ausführen verfügbar sind, streng getrennt von Open Source.
- **Open Source AI Definition.** German Open-Source-KI-Definition (proposal). The definition requiring the freedoms to use, study, modify and share an AI system together with access to the preferred form for modification, including sufficient information about training data, source code and model parameters. *Source.* CLARIAH, "The Current LLM Capability Landscape" with footnote 19. *Literature.* Open Source Initiative, The Open Source AI Definition 1.0. *Short.* The definition requiring freedoms to use, study, modify and share an AI system plus access to training-data information, source code and parameters. *Begriff.* Open-Source-KI-Definition. *Kurz.* Die Definition, die die Freiheiten verlangt, ein KI-System zu nutzen, zu untersuchen, zu verändern und weiterzugeben, sowie Zugang zu Informationen über Trainingsdaten, zu Quellcode und zu Parametern.
- **Open-Washing.** German Open-Washing (proposal). The presentation of an AI system as open where significant restrictions on accessibility or reproducibility remain. *Source.* CLARIAH, "The Current LLM Capability Landscape" with footnote 21. *Literature.* Liesenfeld and Dingemanse 2024, FAccT (DOI 10.1145/3630106.3659005). *Short.* The presentation of an AI system as open while significant restrictions on accessibility or reproducibility remain. *Begriff.* Open-Washing. *Kurz.* Die Darstellung eines KI-Systems als offen, obwohl erhebliche Einschränkungen von Zugänglichkeit oder Reproduzierbarkeit bestehen.
- **Local Deployment.** German lokaler Betrieb (proposal); the German Skriptum does not treat deployment. Running model inference on hardware controlled by the researcher or the institution, which can increase control over research data, inference configuration and availability while shifting responsibility for hardware, security, maintenance and operation to the institution. *Source.* CLARIAH, "The Current LLM Capability Landscape" with footnote 23; Notes EN, "Which Frontier Models and AI Technologies Do You Use?". *Literature.* No canonical source; the scripts name Ollama and LM Studio as environments. *Short.* Running model inference on hardware the researcher or institution controls, which can increase control over data and configuration while shifting operational responsibility to the institution. *Begriff.* lokaler Betrieb. *Kurz.* Der Betrieb von Modellinferenz auf Hardware unter Kontrolle der Forschenden oder der Institution, der die Kontrolle über Daten und Konfiguration erhöhen kann und die Verantwortung für den Betrieb auf die Institution verlagert.

### Risk and regulation

- **Governance Dilemma of Diffusion and Concentration.** German Governance-Dilemma zwischen Diffusion und Konzentration (proposal). The tension the scripts state for cyber capability, where highly capable open-weight systems can spread offensive capability beyond their developers while proprietary frontier systems concentrate the most capable systems and their infrastructure within a small number of companies and states. The source marks this as the author's own assessment, and the marking is preserved. *Source.* CLARIAH, "Frontier LLMs Asymmetrically Amplify Computational Research", cyber-capability passage. *Literature.* AI Security Institute, *Frontier AI Trends Report*. *Short.* The tension, stated as the author's assessment, that open-weight systems can spread offensive capability while proprietary frontier systems concentrate capability within few companies and states. *Begriff.* Governance-Dilemma zwischen Diffusion und Konzentration. *Kurz.* Die als Einschätzung des Autors markierte Spannung, dass Open-Weight-Systeme offensive Fähigkeiten verbreiten können, während proprietäre Frontier-Systeme die leistungsfähigsten Systeme bei wenigen Unternehmen und Staaten konzentrieren.
- **Critical Cyber Capability.** German kritische Cyberfähigkeiten (proposal). The capability threshold at which a provider can no longer rule out that a model could develop functional zero-day exploits against hardened systems or devise and execute novel end-to-end attack strategies with minimal human direction. *Source.* CLARIAH, "Frontier LLMs Asymmetrically Amplify Computational Research" with footnote 31. *Literature.* OpenAI 2026, "Responding to the Next Frontier of Critical Cyber Capabilities", and the Preparedness Framework it refers to. *Short.* The capability threshold at which a provider cannot exclude a model developing zero-day exploits or running novel end-to-end attacks with minimal human direction. *Begriff.* kritische Cyberfähigkeiten. *Kurz.* Die Fähigkeitsschwelle, ab der ein Anbieter nicht mehr ausschließen kann, dass ein Modell Zero-Day-Exploits entwickelt oder neuartige End-to-End-Angriffe mit minimaler menschlicher Anleitung ausführt.
- **AI Watermarking.** German Wasserzeichen, AI Watermarking (UFL, "AI Watermarking"). A mark meant to make content recognisable as generated by an AI system, which works differently for text and for images. A text watermark is applied during generation, where a secret key together with the preceding tokens yields at each step a pseudorandom value that slightly biases the choice of the next token; the text stays readable while carrying a statistical pattern, and a holder of the key computes a score for a text and compares it with a threshold. Detection grows more reliable with longer and less predictable text, and paraphrasing weakens the pattern. An image watermark is embedded into the finished image, where an encoder hides a short bit string in the pixels and a decoder recovers it from a single image; it is trained to survive common perturbations such as compression and cropping, while targeted attacks and removal networks can defeat it. Signed provenance metadata following the C2PA standard are a second route that holds only while the metadata stay attached. A watermark indicates machine origin, and its absence does not show that a person made the content. Article 50(2) of the AI Act, applicable from 2 August 2026, requires providers of generative systems to mark synthetic output in a machine-readable and detectable form, and watermarks are one building block for that obligation. *Source.* UFL, "AI Watermarking" in the appendix. *Literature.* Dathathri et al. 2024 (DOI 10.1038/s41586-024-08025-4); Bui, Agarwal and Collomosse 2023 (arXiv:2311.18297); Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Art. 50(2). *Short.* A hidden statistical or pixel-level mark indicating AI-generated text or images, detectable with a key and indicative of origin only. *Begriff.* Wasserzeichen. *Kurz.* Verborgene Markierung in Text oder Bild, die auf KI-Erzeugung hinweist und sich mit einem Schlüssel erkennen lässt, ohne die Herkunft zu beweisen.

### EU AI Act vocabulary

The governance vocabulary of the teaching line lives in the VetMedAI workshop script, where module 5 is taught as a substantial block with its own exercise on distilling a knowledge document from the regulation. All eight entries are grounded in that script, in its short-form section "Der EU AI Act in Kurzform" and in the worked knowledge document it contains. Common literature anchor for all eight is Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (OJ L, 2024/1689), https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=OJ:L_202401689.

- **Risk-Based Approach.** German risikobasierter Ansatz. The central organising principle of the Act, a four-tier classification of AI systems into unacceptable risk, high risk, limited risk with transparency obligations, and minimal risk, where the tier determines the intensity of the legal obligations. *Source.* VetMedAI, "Regulatory Architecture" of the worked knowledge document. *Short.* The organising principle of the AI Act, a four-tier classification into unacceptable, high, limited and minimal risk, where the tier determines the intensity of obligations. *Begriff.* risikobasierter Ansatz. *Kurz.* Das Ordnungsprinzip des AI Act, eine vierstufige Einteilung in unannehmbares, hohes, begrenztes und minimales Risiko, wobei die Stufe die Intensität der Pflichten bestimmt.
- **AI Literacy Obligation (Art. 4).** German KI-Kompetenz. The duty to ensure the skills, knowledge and understanding that let providers, deployers and affected persons make informed decisions about AI systems and become aware of opportunities, risks and possible harms. It is one of the two obligations that bind a research institution which uses rather than builds AI systems. *Source.* VetMedAI, "Der EU AI Act in Kurzform" and the definitions appendix. *Short.* The duty to ensure the skills, knowledge and understanding for informed decisions about AI systems, one of two obligations binding a research institution using AI. *Begriff.* KI-Kompetenz. *Kurz.* Die Pflicht, die für informierte Entscheidungen über KI-Systeme nötigen Fähigkeiten, Kenntnisse und das nötige Verständnis sicherzustellen, eine der beiden Pflichten, die eine KI nutzende Forschungseinrichtung binden.
- **Prohibited Practices (Art. 5).** German verbotene Praktiken. The eight absolutely prohibited categories of AI practice, among them manipulation through subliminal or deceptive techniques, exploitation of vulnerabilities, social scoring, predictive policing based solely on profiling, untargeted facial scraping, emotion recognition in workplace and education, biometric categorisation by sensitive attributes, and real-time remote biometric identification in public spaces for law enforcement. It is the second obligation immediately relevant to a using institution. *Source.* VetMedAI, "Der EU AI Act in Kurzform" and the prohibited-practices section of the worked knowledge document. *Short.* The eight absolutely prohibited categories of AI practice, such as manipulation, social scoring and workplace emotion recognition, the second obligation relevant to a using institution. *Begriff.* verbotene Praktiken. *Kurz.* Die acht absolut verbotenen Kategorien von KI-Praktiken, etwa Manipulation, Social Scoring und Emotionserkennung am Arbeitsplatz, die zweite für eine nutzende Einrichtung relevante Pflicht.
- **Provider (Art. 3(3)).** German Provider, Anbieter. A natural or legal person who develops or has developed an AI system or GPAI model and places it on the market or puts it into service under their own name or trademark, whether for payment or free of charge. The decisive criterion is responsibility for development and market placement. *Source.* VetMedAI, "Der EU AI Act in Kurzform" and the definitions appendix. *Short.* A natural or legal person developing an AI system or GPAI model and placing it on the market or into service under their own name. *Begriff.* Anbieter. *Kurz.* Eine natürliche oder juristische Person, die ein KI-System oder GPAI-Modell entwickelt und unter eigenem Namen in Verkehr bringt oder in Betrieb nimmt.
- **Deployer (Art. 3).** German Deployer, Betreiber. A natural or legal person using an AI system under their own authority in a professional capacity, with purely private non-professional use excluded. *Source.* VetMedAI, "Der EU AI Act in Kurzform" and the definitions appendix. *Short.* A natural or legal person using an AI system under their own authority in a professional capacity, excluding purely private non-professional use. *Begriff.* Betreiber. *Kurz.* Eine natürliche oder juristische Person, die ein KI-System in eigener Verantwortung beruflich verwendet, unter Ausschluss rein privater, nicht beruflicher Nutzung.
- **High-Risk System.** German Hochrisiko-System. The classification triggering the most demanding compliance regime, reached through two pathways, a safety component of a product covered by the harmonisation legislation of Annex I and requiring third-party conformity assessment, or a standalone system used in one of the eight sensitive domains of Annex III. A research system reaches it only when it leaves pure research for a regulated application area, for instance by qualifying as a medical device or in vitro diagnostic, or by being used in an Annex III context such as recruitment. *Source.* VetMedAI, "Der EU AI Act in Kurzform" and the high-risk section of the worked knowledge document. *Short.* The classification with the most demanding compliance regime, reached as a safety component under Annex I or a standalone system in an Annex III domain. *Begriff.* Hochrisiko-System. *Kurz.* Die Einstufung mit dem anspruchsvollsten Compliance-Regime, erreicht als Sicherheitskomponente nach Anhang I oder als eigenständiges System in einem Bereich nach Anhang III.
- **Research Exemption (Art. 2(6)).** German Forschungsausnahme. The exemption for AI systems developed and used for the sole purpose of scientific research and development prior to market placement. The critical trigger is the transition from research to deployment rather than the subject matter of the research. *Source.* VetMedAI, scope section of the worked knowledge document and the virology example. *Short.* The exemption for AI systems developed and used solely for scientific research before market placement, ending with the transition to deployment whatever the research subject. *Begriff.* Forschungsausnahme. *Kurz.* Die Ausnahme für KI-Systeme, die ausschließlich für wissenschaftliche Forschung vor dem Inverkehrbringen entwickelt und genutzt werden, und die mit dem Übergang in den Einsatz endet, unabhängig vom Forschungsgegenstand.
- **General-Purpose AI Model (GPAI).** German GPAI-Modell. The separate regulatory category of Chapter V, whose providers owe technical documentation, information to downstream providers, a copyright compliance policy and a sufficiently detailed summary of training data content, with additional obligations where systemic risk is presumed, which the Act ties to cumulative training computation above 10²⁵ floating-point operations or to Commission designation. *Source.* VetMedAI, GPAI sections of the worked knowledge document. *Short.* The separate Chapter V category, whose providers owe documentation, downstream information, a copyright policy and a training-data summary, with further obligations under presumed systemic risk. *Begriff.* GPAI-Modell. *Kurz.* Die eigene Regulierungskategorie von Kapitel V, deren Anbieter technische Dokumentation, Informationen für nachgelagerte Anbieter, eine Urheberrechts-Policy und eine Zusammenfassung der Trainingsdaten schulden, mit weiteren Pflichten bei vermutetem systemischem Risiko.
