## 2. The master slide deck text

`Knowledge, Context and Agentic Engineering for Knowledge Work. Full Slidedeck. September 2026..txt` is the plain-text export of the master Google Slides deck, taken minutes before the master script. It is the slide side of the same state and the two should be read together.

Structure. The file opens with a title block, an author block and the AI-assistance disclaimer, then runs the deck linearly without slide numbers. Slide boundaries are recoverable from the short title lines. Roughly two thirds of the running text are German, with English slide titles, English prompt blocks and English source lines. Several slides are visibly unfinished, including a `todo` under Ablauf, a `todo` under Globale CLAUDE.md, three placeholder lines under Knowledge & Context Engineering ("A defintion knowledge", "A definition context engineer", "literatur"), and a slide reading "Hier würde die verbesserte Definition stehen". The deck ends with an Anhang section holding the agent-history material.

Coverage against the five master modules:

- **Understanding LLMs.** Present in the deck as the Jagged Alien Intelligences slide, the benchmark and asymmetric-amplification opener, the Zentrale Begriffe slide and the context-window arithmetic. The deep architecture material (transformer, pretraining and posttraining, the Wikipedia-article-about-zebras figure, tokenisation, embeddings) exists in the master script but has no matching slide block in this export, so the deck is thinner here than the script.
- **Prompt Engineering.** Present as the opening block (definition, shift in focus to context engineering, prompting strategies, latent program space) and again as the Persona Engineering slide with the workshop-participant persona. The Schulhoff citation carries the definition.
- **Knowledge and Context Engineering.** The largest block. Context window arithmetic, context rot, selecting and structuring and distilling knowledge, knowledge documents and their properties, Markdown as serialisation, project knowledge base against working context, the Obsidian vault as a working environment, knowledge modelling and personal information management and project management side by side, the As-a-I-want-to user stories, and the Promptotyping sphere with Scholar-Centred Design and Spec Driven Development.
- **Agentic Engineering.** Fully built out. The AI harness with its schematic, Claude Code as harness, the five-step hands-on arc (Preparation, Planning, Feedback and Self Revision, Implementation, Verification) with complete prompt texts, tool use, `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` including a project-specific skeleton, agent skills, MCP, A2A, subagents, model routing, and the pre-LLM agent history with Wooldridge, reinforcement-learning agents, the Semantic Web agent vision and Sapkota.
- **Critical Perspectives and Governance.** Thinnest module in this export. It appears as the critical-expert framing inside the verification step, the sycophancy and confabulation lines on the Jagged slide, the vibe-coding quotes, and a closing Wiener slide. Evaluation, verification, validation and acceptance are developed in the master script but carry no dedicated slide block here. The ÖAW winter-school text is the richer existing source for this module, since its Block 5 states research integrity, epistemic asymmetry, infrastructure concentration and energy consumption as a stance running through the whole workshop.

Practical consequence. The deck text is usable as the input for `slides/` for four of the five modules. The governance module and the LLM-architecture block both need slide material that does not yet exist, and both have prose sources available (master script for the architecture block, ÖAW text and the VetMedAI EU AI Act block for governance).

## 4. VetMedAI

`Workshop 1. VetMedAI. Grundlagen GenAI und Prompt Engineering. VedMedAI.txt` is the slide text of a German-language workshop held on 2026-04-22 for a veterinary-medicine university in Vienna, framed as AI-competence building for staff. The name "Workshop 1" and the sequence of the material indicate it is the first unit of a series rather than a standalone event.

Content. An opening survey block (which tools do you use, what is your biggest challenge), then an LLM-fundamentals block that is the most complete German version of that material anywhere in this folder (how LLMs work, transformer architecture, pre-training against post-training, the shape of a Wikipedia article about zebras, tokenisation, embeddings with the dog-cat-stone example, the Shakespearean-English illustration, context window arithmetic, context rot, a limitations list covering arithmetic, spelling, sycophancy, absent internal verification, hallucination and data protection). Then prompt engineering with the emotional-prompting examples, the six-part prompt structure, chain of thought against reasoning models, and a worked spreadsheet example. Then Markdown and knowledge documents. Then a large EU AI Act block covering Article 4 AI literacy and Article 3(3) definitions, followed by a hands-on that has participants build a knowledge document about the AI Act, with a complete model solution running to roughly 280 lines covering the risk tiers, actor obligations, general-purpose AI models, governance, enforcement, the application timeline and three reference annexes. It closes with a short AI-agents block.

Recommendation. Register it as a fourth workshop instance. Three reasons carry the decision. It is a real taught instance with a date, an audience and its own profile, which is exactly what the registry models. Its LLM-fundamentals block is the German counterpart to the architecture sections of the master script, which currently exist in English only, so it closes a language gap in the master rather than only adding an instance. Its EU AI Act material is the single largest governance asset in the folder and gives the thinnest master module a concrete anchor, including a reusable hands-on.

Two conditions. The workshop was held in April, so its status in the registry is past rather than planned, and the registry schema currently only shows `planned` in use. And the AI Act model solution names a version and date of its own, so it belongs in the corpus as a dated artefact rather than as a maintained master module.

The ÖAW AI Winter School text deserves the same treatment for the same structural reason. It is a taught instance from February 2026 with full speaker notes, a five-block structure, an explicit thesis (asymmetric amplification), and a reflection block that is the best available source for critical perspectives and governance. It was co-taught, which the registry has no field for yet.
