# Workshop Profile AI Academy 2026

## Profile

This instance is the five-day block week registered for 2026-11-23 to 2026-11-27 at the Veterinärmedizinische Universität Wien, taught in German for administration and research staff. The register describes it as a five-day extended derivation of the complete material and as a follow-up format to the VetMedAI programme, whose first workshop was held in German at the same institution as staff AI-competence building. According to the teaching concept in the author's research vault, last revised on 2026-10-26 and marked there as a draft for coordination, the week divides into two teaching days and three hackathon days. Day 1 covers the foundations of generative AI and the setup, day 2 Promptotyping on a shared synthetic example, and days 3 to 5 the participants' own pre-scoped cases, alone or in teams, with supplementary inputs and a closing demonstration of an own change, its check and its maintained knowledge. The group is heterogeneous in prior knowledge, role and field, from researchers with experience to clinicians and administration staff without programming knowledge; programming experience is not required. The applications are static and run locally in the browser under the data principle of the academy. The operator set the display title *AI Academy* on 2026-09-11, replacing the provisional *VetMed Winter School*, and moved the instance from 2026-11-30 to 2026-11-23. The register in `data/workshops.json` holds the entry and currently carries no deck or script surface. This profile states the planned shape of the instance; a settled content state will exist once the slides and notes for the week are written.

## Planned module selection

Provisional. The selection below maps the vault's day structure onto the five modules and has not been confirmed by the operator. A unit-level cut is open, and the fine module cut in `knowledge/drafts/module-map.md` is itself a draft.

| Module | Planned role |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Understanding Large Language Models | Day 1: current developments, the LLM landscape and how LLMs work. The German LLM-fundamentals material of the VetMedAI series is the closest existing source in the language of this instance. |
| 2 Prompt Engineering | Day 2: prompt and context engineering, demonstrated with a vague and a precise prompt on the same data. |
| 3 Knowledge and Context Engineering | Day 2 and the hackathon: Promptotyping, knowledge documents with Markdown and YAML frontmatter, synthetic data derived from the own case. The knowledge-document exercise of the VetMedAI series is the existing pattern. |
| 4 Agentic Engineering | Day 1 setup, day 2 introduction to the AI harness and local LLMs, and the hackathon days, in which a coding agent works on the own case. |
| 5 Critical Perspectives and Governance | Day 1 in compact form, social, ecological, legal and sovereignty aspects, and the binding data rules of the academy, including the shielding of sensitive data from the coding agent; verification recurs in the hackathon. The vault concept carries no separate EU AI Act block, so that emphasis of the VetMedAI series is not planned. |
