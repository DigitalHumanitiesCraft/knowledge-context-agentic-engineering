## Workshop-specific content

**Hersch material.** The script introduces the Jeanne Hersch workflow, developed with the Zentralbibliothek Zürich for the digital republication of Hersch's writings, as case material in AI-Supported Research Data Workflows & Epistemic Infrastructures, with document OCR, local layout analysis, TEI transformation and entity enrichment, and it links the project frontend and the underlying repository. The executable hands-on package that works on this material lives outside this repo in `chpollin/zbz-ocr-tei` under `workshops/clariah-at-2026/`; `lecture-notes.md` does not spell out its procedure.

**Zweig hands-on.** Both hands-on sections the script does carry work on Stefan Zweig material. Hands-on 1 uses page 1 of the manuscript *Radiovortrag über Newyork* from the Literaturarchiv Salzburg, with a reusable prompt skeleton, a catalogue metadata block for the context section, an added rule against reconstructing readings from linguistic context, and a critical review of the generated transcription against the facsimile. Hands-on 2 takes the transcription produced there as input for information extraction against an explicit schema, so the two representational transitions, facsimile to transcription and transcription to structured information, stay separable with their own failure modes and acceptance criteria.

**Schedule.** The script states none. It is written as continuous lecture notes whose section order is the taught sequence, with the two hands-on sections positioned after the prompt-engineering block and after the agentic-engineering block. The register fixes the date as 2026-09-25; timings, breaks and the placement of the unit inside the summer school week are not fixed in any artefact of this profile.

**Venue context.** The unit is one contribution to the CLARIAH-AT Summer School 2026 *Machine Learning for Digital Scholarly Editions*.
