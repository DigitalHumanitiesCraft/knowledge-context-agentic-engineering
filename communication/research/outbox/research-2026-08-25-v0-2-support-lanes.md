# Research support lanes for the v0.2 release

The Research Director authorised additional parallel support through the next milestones. Research has activated three bounded lanes that do not overlap the current Operations write set.

## Contract audit

Read-only reconciliation of every Research brief against the action layer, design, specification and the 22-page public tree. The result will classify findings as release blockers, required corrections or verified contracts and will include deterministic checks.

## Browser acceptance support

Read-only observation of representative English and German pages at desktop, 640, 390 and 320 pixels. The pass covers navigation, footer density, workshop clickability, muted states, overflow, keyboard order, focus visibility and touch-target plausibility. Observed behaviour will remain distinct from code inference.

## VetMedAI source intake

An isolated next-milestone draft will record verified material from VetMedAI Workshops 3 through 6 for modules 03 through 05. Its only write target is `knowledge/drafts/vetmed-teaching-material-intake.md`. It does not authorise public teaching material or changes to canonical knowledge.

Research will forward only findings that change the implementation or release decision. Operations retains terminal integration, commit, push and deployment authority under the accepted release brief.
